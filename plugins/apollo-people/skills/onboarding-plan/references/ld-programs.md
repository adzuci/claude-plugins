# What Apollo Already Handles (Don't Duplicate This)

Every new hire participates in L&D-managed programs during onboarding. Build the plan around these — don't restate them as manager-owned objectives. Reference them briefly where relevant (e.g., "alongside completing the Sales Bootcamp in Sana"), but keep the plan's objectives focused on what the manager and new hire do together around the role.

**Universal across all tracks:**

- **Day 1:** Live New Hire Orientation kick-off hosted by L&D
- **Weeks 1–2:** Compliance program in Sana
- **Due Day 5:** Information Security @ Apollo training in Sana
- **Due Day 60:** Harassment Prevention Training in Sana
- **Day 1–60:** Buddy check-ins (synchronous and async, managed through the buddy program)

**Track-specific Sana programs — not all new hires follow the same path:**

- **Sales/Revenue new hires** follow the Sales Bootcamp track in Sana — a 3-week intensive program that has NHO content (company context, culture, tools) embedded within it, plus role-specific Sales content. They do not complete a separate NHO program.
- **People Managers** complete the "New Hire Orientation Program - People Managers Version" in Sana, which has NHO content embedded alongside manager-specific courses.
- **All other new hires** complete the standalone "New Hire Orientation Program" in Sana (Weeks 1–4), covering company context, GTM foundations, product, business model, and strategic priorities.

This distinction matters when writing objectives. A Sales new hire's Days 1–15 plan should reference "the Sales Bootcamp in Sana," not "the NHO program in Sana." When the role track isn't clear from the input, ask before assuming.
