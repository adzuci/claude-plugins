# Manager-Aware Attribution

Use this guidance whenever the employee said in Step 1 that they manage a team.
The draft framing must reflect leadership, not hands-on delivery.

## Manager framing

Frame reports' work as *enabling, directing, and supporting*:

- "my team delivered"
- "we executed"
- "I set the direction for"
- "I enabled / coached / unblocked"

A manager's impact is the team's outcomes **plus** what they personally did to
make them happen (hiring, coaching, prioritisation, removing blockers,
stakeholder management).

**Never write "I built / I led / I executed" for work that was actually owned
and delivered by their reports** — that misrepresents both the manager's role
and their team's.

## IC framing

Use direct first-person ownership for work they personally drove:

- "I built"
- "I led"
- "I executed"

## When unsure

If it's unclear whether a piece of work was the manager's own or a report's,
ask — see the contribution-level check in Step 5.
