# Apollo Reference Links — ACE Mid-Year Review

Centralised list of external resources the skill needs to consult during a
review. Update this file when links change — the main `SKILL.md` should not
hardcode these URLs.

## Company OKR Directory

Start here to understand what the employee was accountable for. These sheets list
company-wide OKRs, DRI assignments, and links to team-level KR tracking documents.

- **FY27 Q1 OKRs (1 Feb – 30 Apr 2026):** https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ZgIqAwUv4A53Wl4m2bf-mKgzAqhE-dBqFX0N20s7Cd0/edit?gid=1400704651#gid=1400704651
- **FY27 Q2 OKRs (1 May – 31 Jul 2026):** https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ZgIqAwUv4A53Wl4m2bf-mKgzAqhE-dBqFX0N20s7Cd0/edit?gid=170691627#gid=170691627

Search for the employee's name and their team. Follow any linked tracking docs to
find specific KR progress. When following linked docs, check that the data shown
is from the correct calendar period (February–July 2026) — some trackers show
rolling figures or cumulative data that may include periods outside the review window.

## Apollo Career Framework

**Primary reference:** [`plugins/apollo-people/references/career-framework/company-framework.md`](../../../references/career-framework/company-framework.md)

This file contains the live company baseline framework, organised by level and track (IC vs Manager). Use it to find the employee's current and next-level expectations.

**Which framework to use:**

- **No department-specific framework:** Use the company baseline above. It applies to all departments until department-specific versions are published (expected Q3 2026).
- **Department-specific framework exists:** Ask the employee to upload or paste it, or to provide the current-level and next-level expectations for their specific role/track. Department-specific frameworks take precedence for Growth, Development & Mastery framing.

Find the employee's track (IC or manager) and their stated level. Note:

- Expected competencies and scope at their current level
- Next-level competencies (to inform Growth, Development & Mastery framing)

Use this to ensure achievements and development areas are framed at the right scope and complexity.
