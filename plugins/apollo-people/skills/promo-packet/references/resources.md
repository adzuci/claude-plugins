## Feedback Provider Outreach Template

Use this when messaging feedback providers in Slack. Adapt the bracketed fields. Never send them the pack itself.

```
Hi [Name], I'm putting together a promotion case for [Nominee], currently [Current Level/Title], being considered for [Target Level/Title]. You've worked closely with them on [Projects].

Two things would help:

1. Why do you support this promotion? Specific examples of impact, ownership, or mastery you've seen firsthand carry more weight than general praise.
2. Any risks you'd flag: gaps, timing concerns, anything [Nominee] should work on at the target level.

For what impact, ownership, and mastery look like by level, see the Career Framework here: https://app.notion.com/p/d2efa8fd85d74f96923d90f8b3b92017

A few sentences on each is plenty. Could you get this to me by [date]?

Thanks
```

**Who to ask:** Minimum three people, weighted toward managers — at most one IC. A strong mix: the nominee's skip-level manager, a senior cross-functional collaborator, and a senior stakeholder with direct visibility into their impact.
