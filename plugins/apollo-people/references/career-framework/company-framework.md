# Apollo Career Framework — Company Baseline

**Last synced from Notion:** July 2026
**Source:** [Apollo Career Framework — Data Model](https://app.notion.com/p/3b123c02bca44b05a0115b39a5e98b6d)
**Status:** Live. Which framework to use depends on your department: if your department has no existing framework, use this company baseline now. If your department already has its own framework, continue using that until your department-specific version (built on this spine) rolls out in Q3 2026.

The framework runs on three dimensions — **Impact**, **Ownership**, and **Mastery** — over our **Values**, which are a foundational gate at every level, not a graded dimension.

- **Impact:** What you deliver (Scope of Delivery + Scope of Influence)
- **Ownership:** How you deliver (Autonomy + Complexity)
- **Mastery:** How you raise the bar in your craft (Craft — or Leadership Craft for people managers)

Each level also has one **Level Question**: a single yes/no check for whether you're genuinely operating at that level. A promotion case must be able to answer the target level's question with a clear yes.

Levels run L3–L13 across two tracks. Choose the relevant track for the person you're working with.

## IC Track

### L3 — Entry

**Level Question:** Are you executing assigned tasks reliably, learning from your mistakes, and producing dependable work?

#### Impact

**Scope of Delivery:** Personal contribution to team outcomes (Team KRs). Completes assigned tasks on time as scoped, with manager support. Shows growing consistency in quality and throughput.

**Scope of Influence:** Team and immediate manager. Builds stable working relationships with teammates and your supervisor. Exchanges information to get work done. Collaboration is internal and familiar.

#### Ownership

**Autonomy:** Asks "what should I do?" Engages, doesn't go silent. Stays in contact when uncertain, and asks before going off course, not after.

**Complexity:** Routine, well-defined problems. Applies clear, readily available solutions with limited judgment.

#### Mastery

**Craft:** Sharpens with feedback. Builds reliable habits and uses AI to close skill gaps faster, improving quality with each cycle.

______________________________________________________________________

### L4 — Developing

**Level Question:** Are you delivering strong work independently and starting to shape how work gets done, not just whether it gets done?

#### Impact

**Scope of Delivery:** Individual outcomes (Personal KRs) that contribute to team outcomes (Team KRs). Delivers reliably without being chased. Work doesn't need to be redone.

**Scope of Influence:** Team and adjacent teams. Builds productive working relationships beyond your immediate team. Engages cross-team contacts on routine dependencies. Begins establishing presence outside your direct group.

#### Ownership

**Autonomy:** Makes a recommendation. Brings solutions, not just problems. Flags blockers with a proposed resolution. Doesn't wait to be chased for status.

**Complexity:** Varied but routine problems. Reviews a range of factors, exercises judgment within defined procedures, and makes informed trade-offs.

#### Mastery

**Craft:** Self-corrects before review. Produces consistent work without close oversight, using AI to raise the quality of outputs. Documents what works so others can reuse it.

______________________________________________________________________

### L5 — Career

**Level Question:** Are you owning large-sized projects end-to-end, unblocking others, and navigating ambiguity without being told what to do?

#### Impact

**Scope of Delivery:** Components of team outcomes (Team KRs). Owns large-sized projects end-to-end. Measures and articulates how your work drives team outcomes.

**Scope of Influence:** Senior partners outside your team. Adapts your style for unfamiliar audiences. Others seek your input on decisions beyond your immediate scope.

#### Ownership

**Autonomy:** "I intend to…" Declares intent and proceeds without waiting for sign-off. Acts as an unblocker for yourself and others, keeping work moving.

**Complexity:** Diverse, ambiguous problems. Drives clarity independently, devises solutions from limited information, and adapts existing approaches to new situations.

#### Mastery

**Craft:** Reviews and elevates team work. Produces consistently high-quality work. Uses AI as a working tool, and your outputs reflect it.

______________________________________________________________________

### L6 — Advanced

**Level Question:** Are you acting decisively on the highest-impact work, bringing ideas to the table, and leading cross-team collaboration without needing direction?

#### Impact

**Scope of Delivery:** Team outcomes, end-to-end (Team KRs). Drives multi-team delivery and holds the team's commitments. Translates strategic context into what the team executes against.

**Scope of Influence:** Department-level partners. Leads cross-team collaboration. Trusted across adjacent teams and brought into decisions outside your own. Others seek you out for your perspective.

#### Ownership

**Autonomy:** Do it and report immediately. Acts on the highest-impact work without being directed. Owns team-scope decisions and outcomes, resolving blockers rather than just surfacing them.

**Complexity:** Complex problems requiring in-depth evaluation. Determines methods and procedures on new assignments and exercises judgment on cross-team matters.

#### Mastery

**Craft:** Raises the bar for your team. Reviews and elevates others' work with deep domain expertise. Improves how the team works, including AI-augmented practices.

______________________________________________________________________

### L7 — Expert

**Level Question:** Are you influencing roadmaps and strategy within your department and creating the conditions for multiple teams to operate better?

#### Impact

**Scope of Delivery:** Department outcomes (Dept KRs). Leads multi-quarter, cross-team initiatives that shape how the department delivers against its goals.

**Scope of Influence:** Function-level stakeholders and leadership. Influences cross-department direction and roadmaps. Known by leaders outside your team. Influences decisions beyond your direct scope.

#### Ownership

**Autonomy:** Do it and report periodically. Takes extreme ownership at department scope. Drives outcomes, reports on cadence, and prioritises based on department needs.

**Complexity:** Novel problems without established precedent. Defines the approach before executing it, bridging strategic and technical, and anticipates consequences before they surface.

#### Mastery

**Craft:** Sets the standard for your department. Shapes best practices across the department and adjacent teams with deep domain expertise, including AI-augmented ways of working.

### L8 — Principal

**Level Question:** Are you defining the technical or strategic vision for your function and setting direction others follow?

#### Impact

**Scope of Delivery:** Function outcomes (Function KRs). Sets long-term direction aligned to company strategy, shaping how the function delivers at scale.

**Scope of Influence:** Function leadership and cross-department stakeholders. Shapes the direction function leaders set, and earns recognition as a thought leader across your function.

#### Ownership

**Autonomy:** Drives extreme ownership across the function. Sets how the function operates, defining the approach before others see the problem. Creates the conditions for others to own their work fully.

**Complexity:** Undefined, open-ended problems. Shapes the question before solving it, working through intangible variables with conceptual thinking at function scale.

#### Mastery

**Craft:** Function-level craft authority. Sets the standard for craft excellence across the function. AI-augmented working patterns define what great looks like, and others replicate this approach.

### L9 — Sr. Principal

**Level Question:** Are you driving strategic outcomes across multiple functions and defining how your domain operates at scale?

#### Impact

**Scope of Delivery:** Cross-functional outcomes (Cross-function KRs). Delivers high-ambiguity initiatives that shift how multiple functions operate at scale across the company.

**Scope of Influence:** Leadership Team. Advises senior leadership as a trusted voice, shaping decisions well beyond your function. Earns recognition as a thought leader across the company.

#### Ownership

**Autonomy:** Drives extreme ownership across multiple functions. Sets the strategic agenda, naming the problem and defining the approach before others do. Sets the operating tempo without waiting for permission.

**Complexity:** Ambiguous problems spanning functions. Identifies problems before others see them and sets the company-level agenda, bridging technical insight with business goals.

#### Mastery

**Craft:** Company-level craft authority. Shapes craft and technical direction across the company. Defines how AI is applied in the domain, and the approach becomes the company's internal reference.

### L10 — Distinguished

**Level Question:** Are you defining direction for a company domain with lasting, company-wide influence?

#### Impact

**Scope of Delivery:** Company Objectives within your domain. Sets the direction for a company-level domain that others build against. Few employees exist at this level.

**Scope of Influence:** E-Staff. Shapes company strategy directly as the recognised expert voice at executive level. Earns external recognition as an authority in your domain.

#### Ownership

**Autonomy:** Defines the extreme ownership standard for the domain. Creates conditions where accountability is structural and self-sustaining across the org.

**Complexity:** Abstract problems with no precedent. Defines direction where the rules haven't been written, working on problems that were unnamed before you named them.

#### Mastery

**Craft:** Defines what world-class craft looks like at Apollo. Architects how AI is embedded in systems and ways of working. Not just using AI, but defining how work gets done with it.

## Manager Track

### L6 — Team Lead / Manager

**Level Question:** Are you making outcomes happen through other people, setting context, holding accountability, and owning results you didn't personally produce?

#### Impact

**Scope of Delivery:** Team outcomes (Team KRs). Translates strategic goals into measurable team outcomes. Prioritises the few most impactful goals.

**Scope of Influence:** Department-level partners. Represents your team's commitments, capacity, and priorities to adjacent teams and peer managers. Joins cross-team decisions beyond your immediate scope more and more.

#### Ownership

**Autonomy:** Do it and report immediately. Acts decisively on team-scope decisions without direction. Owns the outcome, not the task, and holds the team accountable through clear DRIs and cadences.

**Complexity:** Complex problems requiring in-depth evaluation. Determines how the team delivers and exercises judgment across people, priorities, and rhythm.

#### Mastery

**Leadership Craft:** Coaches, not just manages. Gives specific feedback and inspects work quality. Makes AI a standard part of how the team does its best work.

______________________________________________________________________

### L7 — Manager II

**Level Question:** Are you building the habits, norms, and systems that make a team consistently deliver on its own?

#### Impact

**Scope of Delivery:** Department outcomes (Dept KRs). Keeps delivery consistent across the department. Coordinates priorities and resourcing so work stays aligned and moving.

**Scope of Influence:** Function-level stakeholders and leadership. Aligns peer managers and department leadership across teams. Drives cross-department decisions and planning.

#### Ownership

**Autonomy:** Do it and report periodically. Takes extreme ownership at department scope. Reports on cadence and keeps leaders informed on results, not process. The area delivers without depending on you.

**Complexity:** Novel problems without established precedent. Frames the operating approach before executing it and balances competing, credible paths.

#### Mastery

**Leadership Craft:** Scales through people. Changes how people work, not just alongside them. Raises the floor on judgement, AI capability, and operating discipline.

______________________________________________________________________

### L8 — Senior Manager

**Level Question:** Are you building an operating system that makes complex work across your area clear, accountable, and consistently delivered?

#### Impact

**Scope of Delivery:** Function outcomes (Function KRs). Delivers complex work across the function and its cross-functional dependencies. Builds operating systems that outlast any single project.

**Scope of Influence:** Function leadership and cross-department stakeholders. Guides function leaders on function-level health and cross-team decisions.

#### Ownership

**Autonomy:** Drives extreme ownership across the function. Sets the direction for how the function operates, defining the operating approach before others see the problem, then acts.

**Complexity:** Undefined, open-ended problems. Shapes the operating approach before the path is set, exercising judgment across the function where ownership is unclear.

#### Mastery

**Leadership Craft:** Builds leaders, not just teams. Plans career development and succession rather than reacting to gaps. Creates a culture of trust, ownership, and AI-augmented working the function needs to deliver consistently.

### L9 — Director

**Level Question:** Are you creating and delivering complex cross-functional work as the driver, not a participant?

#### Impact

**Scope of Delivery:** Cross-functional outcomes (Cross-function KRs). Creates and delivers complex, cross-functional work using resources outside direct control. The trajectory of your domain shifts because of how you operate.

**Scope of Influence:** Leadership team. Represents your domain to the leadership team and makes sound calls without escalating. Drives alignment across multiple functions.

#### Ownership

**Autonomy:** Drives extreme ownership across multiple functions. Sets the strategic agenda, naming the problem and defining the approach before others do. Sets the operating tempo; doesn't wait for permission or process.

**Complexity:** Ambiguous problems spanning functions. Translates ambiguity into executable plans across people, systems, and priorities.

#### Mastery

**Leadership Craft:** Develops leadership capability at scale. Sets clear decision rights and delegation so leaders own outcomes without constant escalation. Drives AI capability and learning as a structural, function-wide standard.

______________________________________________________________________

### L10 — Senior Director

**Level Question:** Are you delivering company-critical cross-functional initiatives with significant ambiguity and limited executive scaffolding?

#### Impact

**Scope of Delivery:** Company direction within the function domain (Cross-function KRs). Delivers against company-critical cross-functional initiatives with significant ambiguity and without executive scaffolding.

**Scope of Influence:** E-Staff. Advises E-Staff on what the company needs in your domain as a decision-making peer, not just a functional expert.

#### Ownership

**Autonomy:** Defines the extreme ownership standard for the domain. Creates conditions where accountability is structural and self-sustaining. Leadership reporting is exception-based; judgment stays at this level, not delegated upward.

**Complexity:** Abstract problems with no precedent. Determines the question before the approach on low-precedent calls with direct effect on company strategy.

#### Mastery

**Leadership Craft:** Builds the leadership bench. Reduces key-person risk; strong leaders grow and stay. Leadership development is structural, not personal, and AI capability is built into the pipeline, not left to chance.

### L11 — VP

**Level Question:** Are you impacting or altering the strategy of the company, driving significant improvements in key business metrics?

#### Impact

**Scope of Delivery:** Company outcomes at scale (Company KRs). Drives Company KR delivery across functions. The delivery system works at scale without constant executive input.

**Scope of Influence:** E-Staff peer; influence reaching the Core team. Drives the strategic agenda E-Staff focuses on, not just earns their trust. Core-team engagement is used to set direction.

#### Ownership

**Autonomy:** Drives extreme ownership at company scale through a proven playbook. Operates a repeatable model that delivers measurable business impact and holds full accountability for company-scale results.

**Complexity:** Company-shaping problems. Synthesises imperfect signals and cross-functional trade-offs under low precedent and high ambiguity.

#### Mastery

**Leadership Craft:** Shapes the talent agenda. Builds leadership capability for the company's pipeline, not just the function's bench. Holds a clear view on what Apollo needs from its leaders, including AI capability.

### L12 — SVP

**Level Question:** Are you permanently changing the trajectory of the business, creating long-term lasting impact?

#### Impact

**Scope of Delivery:** Company Objectives across functions. Defines and drives transformational, cross-functional initiatives that reshape how major areas of the company operate.

**Scope of Influence:** Core team. Carries a recognised voice within the Core team and owns the trajectory of a major business area.

#### Ownership

**Autonomy:** Drives extreme ownership at transformational scale by building the playbook others run. Defines the operating model where none exists and proves it at scale across a major business area. The frameworks and standards you set become how the company operates.

**Complexity:** Transformational problems. Relies on judgment, not precedent, with no comparable to draw on for calls that reshape the business.

#### Mastery

**Leadership Craft:** Sets the leadership standard. Builds capability that outlasts individuals; the business area develops people who rise to the top of the company. Models an approach to AI-augmented leadership that becomes the company norm.

### L13 — C-Level

**Level Question:** Are you positively altering the company's trajectory in a way you can point to?

#### Impact

**Scope of Delivery:** Company Objectives, company-wide. Changes the company trajectory. Owns the lowest-precedent decisions that determine what Apollo builds, bets on, and leaves behind.

**Scope of Influence:** Core team and board. Owns how the company operates, performs, and sustains results over the long term.

#### Ownership

**Autonomy:** Defines the ultimate ownership standard for Apollo. Makes the calls no one else is positioned to make: lowest precedent, highest consequence, no escalation path above.

**Complexity:** Highest-order problems. Sets precedent where none exists; the call itself becomes the company's standard.

#### Mastery

**Leadership Craft:** Defines the growth culture. Shapes the organisation's approach to talent, including how it builds AI-native leaders. Grows leaders at every level without depending on this person's direct involvement.

## Notes for Skills Using This File

- **Department-specific competencies are coming in Q3 2026.** When they land, department framework files will be added alongside this one following the same structure. Skills should surface this caveat to users when relevant.
- **Values are a gate, not a dimension.** They apply at every level and are not graded. Don't frame them as something that scales with promotion.
- **Use the Level Question as the primary anchor.** When helping with a promotion case, career conversation, or self-review, the Level Question for the relevant level is the clearest single check of whether someone is genuinely operating there.
- **IC vs Manager track matters for Mastery.** Manager-track Mastery is Leadership Craft (people, systems, development). IC-track Mastery is domain/technical craft. Always confirm track before referencing Mastery expectations.
