# apollo-people

People Enablement skills for Apollo employees — performance reviews, career development, and growth tools.

## Skills

- **`ace-mid-year-review`** — Helps an Apollo employee draft their ACE mid-year self-review for submission in CultureAmp. Acts as a thinking partner, searches connected tools (Jira, Notion, Slack, Google Drive) for evidence, and produces a first-person draft across the Impact, Ownership, and Growth & Mastery sections. Requires Notion, Slack, Google Drive, and Jira connectors.

Each skill's full trigger phrases, scope, and workflow live in its own `SKILL.md`.
