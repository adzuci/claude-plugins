**Last verified: 2026-08-11, source: Apollo one-sheets.** These proof stats
come from marketing collateral that gets updated periodically — if this date
is more than a couple months old when you're reading this, spot-check the
figures below against the current one-sheets before citing them, since they
may have drifted.

Use this to answer template question 2 ("Where can we create value?") and to
build the value hypothesis. Match the customer's observed business priority (from
Salesforce, usage, and web research) to the relevant Apollo capability, and cite
the proof stat. Do not staple on a value prop the research doesn't support —
lead with the customer's priority, then attach the capability that fits it.

Source: Apollo product one-sheets (Data Enrichment, Deal Execution, Inbound,
Outbound). Stats are Apollo's published figures — attribute them as "per Apollo"
in the plan.

## Capability quick reference

- **Data Enrichment** — waterfall enrichment across Apollo + trusted partners,
  real-time intent, job-change alerts, AI lead scoring, CRM/CSV/API enrichment.
- **Outbound** — AI-built multi-channel sequences, deliverability suite, parallel
  dialer, AI-powered research/tasks/follow-ups, analytics.
- **Inbound** — website visitor de-anonymization, form shortening & enrichment,
  inbound router, qualification agent, meeting scheduler.
- **Deal Execution** — meeting scheduler, AI pre-meeting insights, AI call
  recording, automated workflows, deal management, pipeline analytics.

Positioning principle (from the account plan template, page 2): the value
hypothesis marries a *business priority/event* to a *desired outcome* and names
the Apollo *solution* and the resulting *business impact*. This table supplies
the "solution" and "impact"; the research supplies the "priority/event".
