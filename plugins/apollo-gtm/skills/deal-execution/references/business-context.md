## Apollo's Defensible Differentiators

From AOP R&D section: "We own the context, builders, and the rep surfaces."

| Differentiator | Type (CoTM) | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **We own the builders** | Unique | One place to build and maintain motions (sequences + workflows). Competitors fragment this. |
| **We win on reps** | Comparative | Tasks + inbox + dialer are where reps spend their day. Deeper integration than point solutions. |
| **We have the context** | Unique | Rep's owned accounts, history, contacts, activity, and signals. Better than generic AI because of data depth. |
| **Leaders can trust it** | Holistic | Guardrails, visibility, controls. Reduces executive risk of AI adoption. |

Each differentiator should be deployed using the CoTM "How We Do It Better" framework:
- **So what?** — What does this mean for the customer?
- **Says who?** — What proof do you have?
