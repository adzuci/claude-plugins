# MEDDPICC — Qualification Framework

MEDDPICC is the qualification framework. It tells you whether a deal is real and where the gaps are before they become surprises. It is embedded in exit criteria and layered throughout the Sales Process — not required up front.

**Critical rule:** $25K+ deals require full MEDDPICC documentation in SFDC. No exceptions.

---

## Field Definitions

| Field | Letter | Definition | What to Ask | Primary Stage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Metrics** | M | Quantified business impact — how success is measured | "What outcome are you trying to improve, and by how much?" / "What's the current baseline, and what would success look like in 90 days?" | Stage 1 |
| **Economic Buyer** | E | The person who controls budget and final sign-off | "Who ultimately owns the budget and signs off on this investment?" / "What does that person care about most — cost reduction, growth, risk, or speed?" | Stage 2 |
| **Decision Criteria** | D | The factors that matter most in their evaluation | "What are the top 3 criteria you'll use to choose a solution?" / "What would make a solution a clear 'yes' vs a 'no' for you?" | Stage 2 |
| **Decision Process** | D | The steps and people required to get a decision made | "Walk me through how a decision gets made — steps, stakeholders, and timeline." / "What needs to happen internally before you can purchase?" | Stage 3 |
| **Paper Process** | P | The legal, procurement, and security steps to execute a contract | "What does procurement/security/legal look like on your side, and how long does it typically take?" / "Any required approvals or paperwork we should plan for early?" | Stage 4 |
| **Identified Pain** | I | The business problem and cost of inaction | "What's the business problem that prompted you to look at this now?" / "What happens if you don't solve this in the next 3-6 months?" | Stage 1 |
| **Champion** | C | The internal advocate who sells for you when you're not in the room | "Who will own this internally and drive it forward if you're not in the room?" / "If we're successful, what does a win look like for you personally?" | Stage 2-3 |
| **Competition** | C | Who else is being evaluated and how Apollo compares | "What other tools or options are you considering (including doing nothing)?" / "What do you like about the current approach — and what's not working?" | Stage 1-2 |

## Champion vs. Coach Diagnostic

A Champion has three non-negotiable traits:
1. **Power or influence** — they move decisions, not just observe them.
2. **Actively sells on your behalf** — they advocate in rooms you'll never enter.
3. **Personal win** — their career or goals are tied to your deal succeeding.

### Behavioral Signals

| Signal | Champion | Coach |
|---|---|---|
| Forecasting | Predicts outcomes ("here's what's going to happen") | Recaps meetings ("here's what happened") |
| Bad news | Shares it early and proactively | Shares good news only, or surfaces problems late |
| Access to EB | Introduces you directly | Promises intros that stall or don't materialize |
| Decision criteria | Modifies them in your favor | Reports them as-is |
| Business case | Co-builds it with you, contributes their own metrics | Forwards your deck without modification |
| Urgency | Treats policies as guidelines, influences outcomes | Talks about budget limits and company guidelines as blockers |
| Personal stake | Links their success to your solution succeeding | Friendly and helpful but no skin in the game |

### Advocate vs. Champion Trait Table

From Apollo's internal Champion Guide — use between Stage 1 and Stage 2 when you believe you've identified a champion:

| Trait | Advocate | Champion |
|---|---|---|
| Provides information about the company and competition | Yes | Yes |
| Coaches you during the sale | Yes | Yes |
| Has an interest in your winning | Yes | Yes |
| Has influence in the company | No | Yes |
| Gives access to the Economic Buyer | No | Yes |
| Sells for you when you are not in the room | No | Yes |
| Has authority in the account | Maybe | Yes |
| Co-authors the decision criteria | Maybe | Yes |

Mostly Champion traits? You're working with someone who truly drives the deal.

### Champion Confidence Questions

Champions should be able to confidently answer:
- How are purchases made at their company?
- What's the evaluation process?
- Who made recent buying decisions?
- Can they map out their current buying process?
- What are the key pain points and consequences of inaction?
- How does your solution help the business?
- What happened in the competitor meeting?

More "YES" answers = higher likelihood the person is a qualified Champion.

### High-Commitment Tasks

Ask them to complete high-commitment tasks to validate:
- Arrange a demo with another department
- Introduce you to key stakeholders such as the Economic Buyer or Procurement
- Share internal product evaluation documentation
- Sponsor a meeting with the Economic Buyer
- Help craft a perspective

If they complete multiple activities, you're likely working a true internal Champion.

### 7 Champion Validation Tests

1. Does this person have power and influence within their organization?
2. Are they actively selling on your behalf (evidence, not emotion)?
3. Do they share bad news proactively?
4. Will they co-create a mutual close plan?
5. Will they help build the business justification with their own metrics?
6. Can they map the decision and procurement process (who signs, who blocks, what paperwork)?
7. Do they link their personal success to your solution?

### Champion Scoring

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Grey | No champion identified. "Friends" but no evidence of influence or personal win. |
| Yellow | Potential champion. Personal win known but not validated. Access to EB unconfirmed. |
| Green | Champion validated — sponsoring EB meetings, modifying decision criteria, co-building ROI case. |

**Can a coach become a champion?** Yes — if they gain organizational influence or develop a personal stake. Re-run the 7 validation tests before upgrading status.

## Stage-to-MEDDPICC Matrix

| Stage | MEDDPICC Fields Required | $25K+ Additional Requirements | SFDC Fields |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stage 0 (Lead)** | None (BDR stage) | — | Handoff Notes, Initial Meeting Contact, Initial Meeting Date |
| **Stage 1 (Discovery)** | M (Metrics), I (Identified Pain) | Capabilities ($10K+) | Identified Pain, Metrics, Capabilities, ARR, Num Seats, Next Step, Next Step Date, Contract Term |
| **Stage 2 (Qualification)** | E (Economic Buyer), D (Decision Criteria), C (Competition) | Decision Criteria required | Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria ($25K+), Competitor Details |
| **Stage 3 (Solution Eval)** | D (Decision Process), C (Champion) | Champion Name, Decision Process required | Champion Y/N, Champion Name ($25K+), Decision Process ($25K+), Technical Win, ZP Team ID |
| **Stage 4 (Pricing/Negotiation)** | P (Paper Process) | Paper Process required | Paper Process ($25K+), Products Sold, Num Seats, Capabilities Validated, Start/End/Renewal Dates, Payment details |
| **Stage 5 (Out for Signature)** | All fields should be complete | Full MEDDPICC documentation | AE → Onboarding Handoff ($10K+) |
| **Stage 6 (Closed)** | — | — | Loss Reason fields (Closed Lost only) |

## Inspection and Coaching Protocol

- Managers attend/listen to minimum 10 calls per week.
- Coach directly to scorecard gaps.
- AI provides summaries of how coaching is being implemented on calls.
- Reinforce Command of the Message through deeper deal review and pipeline inspection.
