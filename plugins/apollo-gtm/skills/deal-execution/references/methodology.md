# Command of the Message — Sales Methodology

Force Management's value-based sales methodology, implemented at Apollo. Equips sellers to articulate value in the context of the customer's specific challenges, priorities, and desired outcomes.

**Core rule:** "Never lead with a solution until you know the problem. If there isn't a customer need or problem we can solve, the opportunity is not a fit."

---

## Value Conversation Framework

The methodology structures conversations as a deliberate sequence. Each element builds on the previous one. The order matters — skipping ahead weakens urgency and disconnects value from pain.

| # | Element | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Before Scenario** | The customer's current state — what is happening now. Describes real conditions, workflows, pain points in the customer's language. |
| 2 | **Negative Consequences** | What goes wrong if the current situation continues. Business impact of inaction. Where urgency begins. Must be quantified. |
| 3 | **After Scenario** | What the customer's world looks like with the problem solved. Expressed in business terms, not as the inverse of pain. |
| 4 | **Positive Business Outcomes (PBOs)** | The measurable business improvement. Specific: lower operating cost, faster product launch, better retention, reduced compliance risk. |
| 5 | **Required Capabilities** | What the customer must be able to do to reach the future state. Expressed in the customer's language, not vendor features. Bridges pain and solution. Shapes decision criteria. The seller who owns the required capabilities usually wins the deal. |
| 6 | **Metrics** | How the customer defines success. KPIs that show whether required capabilities are working. |
| 7 | **How We Do It** | How the solution delivers the required capabilities. Follows the mantra structure after confirming outcomes, capabilities, and metrics. |
| 8 | **How We Do It Better** | Defensible differentiators. Three types: **unique** (only you have it), **comparative** (others have similar, you do it better), **holistic** (company-level strengths that reduce risk). Each must answer "So what?" (customer value) and "Says who?" (proof). |
| 9 | **Proof Points** | Evidence that supports "how we do it better." Proof of value > proof of concept. Good proof deepens discovery. |
| 10 | **Trap-Setting Questions** | Discovery questions designed to influence required capabilities toward your real differentiators. Flow: relevant differentiator → target required capability → question that helps customer see why it matters. Only works if differentiator is real. |

---

## The Mantra (Repeatable Talk Track)

1. Play back the positive business outcomes the customer wants.
2. Connect those outcomes to the required capabilities they will need.
3. Confirm how success will be measured.
4. Explain how you do it.
5. Explain how you do it better.
6. Support that with proof points.

Template: "What I hear you saying is that these are the outcomes you are trying to achieve. In order to achieve them, these are the capabilities you will need. You will likely measure success in these ways. Let me show you how we help. Let me show you where we are meaningfully different. And let me show you proof."

## The Three Tenets

Apollo's discovery methodology is built around seamlessly uncovering three essential questions behind every deal:

| Tenet | Question | Purpose | Discovery Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Why Buy Anything?** | What is the real business problem? | Establish pain and consequences. Uncover Before Scenario and Negative Consequences. | Map current state, root challenges. "Be a problem finder, not a problem solver." |
| **Why Buy Now?** | What creates urgency? | Uncover internal triggers, timing pressures, change drivers. Use loss aversion framing. | Risk, timeline, decision criteria, decision-making process, negative consequences of delay. |
| **Why Buy Apollo?** | Why is Apollo the right path forward? | Connect Apollo's strengths to the specific outcomes the buyer cares about. Differentiate. | Leverage insights to position as best-fit. Connect value drivers to buyer priorities. |

## Apollo-Specific Discovery Question Bank

### Why Buy Anything

- "Walk me through your data strategy — what's working well, and where do you see inefficiencies?"
- "Can you help me understand how you are currently acquiring the data that powers your products?"
- "Where does your current data fall short of customer needs?"
- "How much time do your teams spend cleaning or validating data today?"
- "Have any recent data quality issues impacted customer satisfaction or churn?"
- "What does your ideal solution look like? How would the customer experience change?"

### Why Buy Now

- "What role does data play in supporting your upcoming product or feature launch?"
- "What's the cost of losing customers due to unreliable or incomplete data?"
- "If this issue isn't solved, what impact will it have on your goals this quarter/year?"
- "What's prompting the evaluation now and when do you ideally want to have a solution in place?"
- "Is there an internal milestone, rollout, or contract date we're working back from?"

### Diagnostic / Competitive Discovery

- If prospect status is "yellow" (competitive situation identified), complete full competitive discovery before advancing.

## Discovery Call Framework (7 Steps)

| Step | Activity | Key Actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Pre-Call Prep | Gather Apollo insights | Potential sources of pain, "why buy now" signals: job postings, exec interviews, press releases, LinkedIn activity, existing Apollo users at the account |
| 2. Call Opening & Agenda | Customer-centric opening | Start with their world, not ours. Reiterate anything learned from form submission or pre-call prep. |
| 3. Current State & KPIs | Why Buy Anything | Map current state and root challenges. Complete competitive discovery if status is "yellow." |
| 4. Risk, Decision Criteria & Timeline | Why Buy Now | Emphasize timing and negative consequences. Uncover change drivers, timeline, decision criteria, decision-making process. Use loss aversion framing. |
| 5. Solution Overview | Why Buy Apollo | Leverage insights to position Apollo as best-fit. Connect value drivers to buyer priorities. |
| 6. Q&A and Next Steps | Lock next meeting | Reserve 5-10 minutes. Confirm alignment. ALWAYS ensure next meeting is scheduled. |
| 7. Follow-Up Email | Within 24hrs max | Brief recap, relevant enablement resources, trial details, quote if applicable, clear next steps. |
