## Solution Features

### ENRICH
Enrich your records with verified contact data, detailed demographic and firmographic data, and real-time intent so you can connect with confidence.
- Constantly refreshed database of 210M+ contacts and 30M+ companies
- Expand and orchestrate data from one place with waterfall enrichment
- Enrich your records with any type of data using the AI Assistant

### QUALIFY
Combine all your data points to find the best-fit leads and use AI to help your reps prioritize reaching out to the right leads at the right time.
- Let AI research and sync custom lead data to your CRM
- See which leads are in-market and most likely to respond
- Use Apollo's AI lead scoring or easily build your own scoring logic

### CLEANSE
Use enriched data to keep your CRM clean, update other tools, and fuel your outbound campaigns.
- Update your entire CRM with accurate data at intervals you control
- Easily import, enrich, and export data using CSV
- Push and pull data into other tools via API and integrations
