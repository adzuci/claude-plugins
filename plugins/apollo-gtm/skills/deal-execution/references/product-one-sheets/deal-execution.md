## Solution Features

### PREP
Make the most of every conversation with automated scheduling and done-for-you prep, so reps can have more effective meetings.
- Eliminate back-and-forth emails with instant calendar booking
- Use AI-powered insights to prep for every call in seconds
- Get prep notes automatically sent to you or attached to your calendar

### PROGRESS
Keep deals moving — automatically. Put note taking and repetitive tasks on autopilot, leaving reps to focus on relationships.
- Capture and summarize calls with AI-powered recording
- Automatically generate tasks, draft recaps, and send follow-up emails with AI Assistant so nothing falls through the cracks
- Keep your CRM in sync using auto-populated conversation insights

### MANAGE
Manage your pipeline and your team with one unified, always-updated source of truth.
- Seamlessly sync, enrich, and organize contacts and deals, so reps can focus on closing
- Stay one step ahead with real-time pipeline visibility and deal analytics
- Coach reps and build best practices based on actual call insights and recorded examples
