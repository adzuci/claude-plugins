# Framework Linkages — Three-Framework Integration

How Command of the Message, the Apollo Sales Process, and MEDDPICC connect at each deal stage.

From the Sales Process Hub:

> "Sales Methodology → Sales Process → Qualification Framework. These three layers are not separate — they're one unified framework — Apollo GTM Execution — and they work together on every deal."

**The relationship in practice:**
- The Sales Process gives you the structure and accountability to know what to focus on at each stage and who to involve.
- CoM gives you the language and questions to run great discovery, deliver compelling demos, and build urgency.
- MEDDPICC gives you the deal health signal to know what you know, what you don't know, and where your deal is most at risk.

All three must work together — a great discovery conversation (CoM) that uncovers real pain (MEDDPICC) and gets documented and acted on (Sales Process) is how Apollo wins.

## Stage 0: Lead (BDR-Owned)

| Framework | Activity | Key Outputs |
|---|---|---|
| **CoTM** | Pre-call research using Apollo insights. Identify potential pain signals and "why buy now" indicators. | Pain hypotheses for AE handoff. |
| **Sales Process** | Persona-aligned outreach. Schedule Director+ meeting within 48hrs. Document handoff notes. | SFDC Stage 0 fields complete. Qualified meeting on calendar. |
| **MEDDPICC** | Not active at this stage. | — |

## Stage 1: Discovery

| Framework | Activity | Key Outputs |
|---|---|---|
| **CoTM** | Run 3 Tenets discovery flow. Diagnose current state + consequences. Map Before/After scenarios. Identify required capabilities. | Before Scenario documented. Negative Consequences quantified. Initial Required Capabilities identified. |
| **Sales Process** | Execute first substantive conversation. Educate on pricing/packaging. Establish MAP. Launch deal room (>$10K SMB / >$20K MM). | SFDC Stage 1 fields complete. MAP initiated. Deal room created. |
| **MEDDPICC** | Identify Pain (I) tied to business impact. Surface Metrics (M) — quantified business impact. Begin Competition (C) discovery. | Pain in buyer's language. Success metrics captured. Competitive landscape initial read. |

**Cross-Framework Validation:**
- CoTM Before Scenario feeds MEDDPICC Identify Pain → same discovery questions serve both.
- CoTM Negative Consequences feed "Why Buy Now" urgency → surfaces compelling events for deal timeline.
- Capabilities field ($10K+) bridges CoTM Required Capabilities and SFDC gating.
- If competitive situation detected ("yellow"), complete full competitive discovery before advancing.

---

## Stage 2: Qualification

| Framework | Activity | Key Outputs |
|---|---|---|
| **CoTM** | Deepen Before/After. Quantify Negative Consequences with dollars. Deploy trap-setting questions to shape evaluation criteria. | Decision criteria shaped toward Apollo differentiators. |
| **Sales Process** | Align on evaluation plan + success criteria. Introduce SC. Schedule internal review meeting. Confirm budget access. | SFDC Stage 2 fields complete. SC engaged. Evaluation plan documented. |
| **MEDDPICC** | Identify Economic Buyer (E). Define Decision Criteria (D) for $25K+. Map Competitor Details (C). Begin Champion identification (C). | EB identified with budget authority. Evaluation criteria documented. Competitive landscape mapped. |

**Cross-Framework Validation:**
- CoTM Required Capabilities → MEDDPICC Decision Criteria. If they diverge, either discovery was weak or a competitor is shaping criteria.
- CoTM Trap-Setting Questions → Competition field. Trap questions expose competitive gaps.
- Champion identification begins here (tested in Stage 3). A real champion co-builds the CoTM business case and influences Decision Criteria in your favor.
- SC enters for technical assessment — validates that Required Capabilities can be delivered.

---

## Stage 3: Solution Evaluation

| Framework | Activity | Key Outputs |
|---|---|---|
| **CoTM** | Present PBOs. Demo Required Capabilities. Execute "How We Do It Better" with proof points. Handle competitive positioning. | Value narrative delivered. Differentiation established. |
| **Sales Process** | Execute evaluation: demo, trial, data duel, pilot. Validate technical requirements. Intro to procurement. Start legal/security review. Engage Apollo Exec. | Technical Win secured. Vendor of Choice confirmed. Path to paper established. |
| **MEDDPICC** | Map Decision Process (D) for $25K+. Validate Champion (C) — Champion Y/N + Champion Name ($25K+). Confirm Technical Win. | Decision process fully mapped. Champion validated and tested. Technical fit confirmed. |

**Cross-Framework Validation:**
- CoTM PBOs → MEDDPICC Metrics. Positive business outcomes should match buyer's success metrics. Misalignment = warning.
- Champion Validation: run the 7 validation tests. A real champion is co-building the business case, not just reporting back.
- SC leads evaluation execution (data duels, trials). Data Duels demonstrate Required Capabilities with prospect's own data.
- If new stakeholders appear → run mini-discovery using 3 Tenets to quickly align.

---

## Stage 4: Pricing and Negotiation

| Framework | Activity | Key Outputs |
|---|---|---|
| **CoTM** | Reinforce value alignment through Before/After framing. Handle objections by reconnecting to PBOs and Required Capabilities. Prevent value erosion. | Value narrative maintained through close. |
| **Sales Process** | Present pricing + packaging. Finalize legal/security/commercials. Align on rollout strategy. Explain Support Model and GTME (>$15K). Maintain MAP. | Commercial terms confirmed. Contract details complete. |
| **MEDDPICC** | Paper Process (P) documented for $25K+. Capabilities validated for deployment. | Procurement/legal/security timeline mapped with owners. |

**Cross-Framework Validation:**
- Paper Process delays → use CoTM Negative Consequences (cost of delay) to maintain urgency with Champion and EB.
- If Decision Criteria shift → revisit CoTM Required Capabilities. Competitor may have reshaped evaluation.
- If Champion goes silent → re-run 7 validation tests. May need secondary champion.
