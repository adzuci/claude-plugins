# SFDC Capabilities Definitions

Use this resource when filling out and validating the Capabilities exit criteria in Stage 1 and Stage 4. Maps use case categories to customer language so you can match what the customer says to what goes in SFDC.

**When to use:** Stage 1 (initial Capabilities submission for $10K+ deals) and Stage 4 (Capabilities validated for deployment).

---

## Outbound Capabilities

| Capability | What the Customer May Say |
|---|---|
| **Sequencing and Deliverability** | "When I'm running outbound, I need to easily create and manage multi-step sequences and ensure my emails reliably land in the inbox so I get more engagement and conversion from my outreach." |
| **Parallel and Power Dialing** | "When I'm calling prospects, I need a faster way to dial through lists so I can spend my time on live conversations, not manual dialing." |
| **AI Messaging** | "When I'm preparing for outreach, I need AI to help me research companies and prospects and turn those ideas into personalized messaging for email and call scripts." |
| **Workflows and Task Management** | "When my day is busy, I need a single queue of actions/tasks to do next and automated workflows that consistently pull new prospects/leads for me so I keep building and moving pipeline forward." |
| **Meeting Scheduling** | "When a prospect is interested, I need them to book time with me in a couple of clicks so I can move to discovery before interest goes cold." |

---

## Inbound Capabilities

| Capability | What the Customer May Say |
|---|---|
| **De-Anonymize Website Visitors** | "When people hit my website, I need to know which companies they're from, what they're interested in, and how strong their buying intent is, so I can prioritize outreach to the warmest accounts." |
| **Form Enrichment** | "When someone fills out a form, I need Apollo to add the missing details for me so I can keep forms short but give sales complete records." |
| **Route and Schedule Meetings** | "When a hot lead comes in, I need it routed and scheduled with the right rep automatically so we respond in minutes, not days." |

---

## Enrichment Capabilities

| Capability | What the Customer May Say |
|---|---|
| **Browser Extension** | "When I'm on LinkedIn or a website, I need to see verified data and add people to Apollo in one click so I can prospect wherever I work." |
| **Data and TAM Enrichment** | "When I look at my market, I need my accounts and contacts kept enriched and up to date so I can size TAM and segment with confidence." |
| **Lead Account and Contact Scoring** | "When I have more leads than my team can touch, I need clear scores on who's most likely to buy so we know who to work first." |
| **AI Research** | "When I'm preparing outreach or a call, I need AI to research the account and contact for me so I can personalize without hours of prep." |
| **Signals and Intent** | "When buyers show signs of interest, I need those signals in one place so I can trigger the right plays at the right time." |

| Capability | What the Customer May Say |
|---|---|

## How to Use in SFDC

1. **Stage 1 ($10K+ deals):** During or after discovery, identify which capabilities match the customer's stated needs. Submit the relevant capabilities in SFDC.
2. **Stage 4:** Validate that the capabilities identified in Stage 1 still match the deployment plan. Update if the use case has evolved.
3. **Mapping tip:** Listen for the customer's language, not Apollo's. The "What the Customer May Say" column helps you match their words to the right capability category.
