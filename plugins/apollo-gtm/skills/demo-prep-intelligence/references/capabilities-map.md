# Apollo Capabilities Map

Last updated: 2026-05-04. Review quarterly or whenever Apollo Product Marketing publishes updated capability messaging. If this file has not been reviewed in the last 90 days, add this caution to any capability-sensitive brief or synthesis: `Capabilities map may be outdated — verify current Apollo capabilities before demo.` If a deal mentions a capability, feature, or product name not in this map, include it in the brief as `Capability not in reference map — verify current availability` rather than forcing it into the nearest existing bucket.

Use this reference to map customer language, pain points, decision criteria, and demo recommendations to Apollo capabilities. Prefer the customer's language over Apollo feature jargon when explaining why to show something.

Internal terminology: "brief mode" and "synthesis mode" are architectural labels for the agent. Never surface these labels to the SC. Translate to plain language ("the brief", "the full synthesis package") in any user-visible output.

## Platform Consolidation

Use this when the deal is about replacing multiple tools, reducing vendor sprawl, or connecting data + engagement + workflow + reporting in one motion. Do not treat consolidation as a generic discount story; show how connected workflows remove operational complexity.

| Consolidation angle | Customer language | Demo implications |
|---|---|---|
| Data + Engagement Consolidation | "We want one platform for finding the right people and engaging them without jumping between tools." | Show a connected flow: build target audience → enrich/verify data → add to sequence → track engagement. |
| Workflow + Sequencing Consolidation | "We need automation that does not require separate reports, manual cadences, and brittle workarounds." | Show workflow trigger → branching/suppression → sequence enrollment → analytics in one motion. |
| Cost and Vendor Complexity Reduction | "We are paying for overlapping tools and still need RevOps to stitch them together." | Frame the demo around fewer handoffs, less ProServe dependency, lower admin burden, and one operating surface. |
| Rep Productivity Platform | "Reps need one place to know who to contact, what to say, and what to do next." | Show account/contact intelligence, AI messaging, tasks, sequences, and rep activity surface together. |

### When to Lead with Platform vs. Feature

- Lead with the platform story when the buyer is replacing two or more tools, has executive cost/complexity pressure, or complains about handoffs between data, engagement, CRM, and reporting.
- Lead with an individual capability when one verified pain dominates the evaluation and consolidation is only a secondary benefit.
- In the full synthesis package, if platform consolidation is the strategic angle, order Apollo AI prompts as a connected workflow rather than separate feature demos.

## Standard Flow Additions

Consider these when not already covered:

- AI Research: usually worth showing when personalization, prep time, or outbound quality appears anywhere in the deal.
- Waterfall Enrichment: usually worth showing when data quality, CRM hygiene, match rate, phone/email coverage, ZoomInfo replacement, or Data Duel appears.
- Workflows: usually worth showing when the buyer cares about repeatability, rep productivity, routing, or operational scale.
- Signals and Intent: usually worth showing when the buyer mentions prioritization, account-based motions, timing, or pipeline efficiency.
- Platform Consolidation: usually worth showing when the buyer is replacing multiple tools, has overlapping spend, or wants data + engagement + automation in one surface.
