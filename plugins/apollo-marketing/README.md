# apollo-marketing

Apollo's brand copilot for all teams. Generates on-brand content, answers brand questions, and enforces Apollo's voice across all channels.

## Skills

- **`brand-agent`** — Apollo's internal AI copywriter. Generates on-brand content across email, web, social, ads, blog, in-product UI, and Reddit. Enforces Apollo's voice pillars (Confident, Clear, Illuminating, Sharp, Human) and copywriting rules. Knows when to route copy or design for human review. Trigger phrases include "write copy for", "help me write", "review this copy", "is this on-brand", "draft an email".

Each skill's full trigger phrases, scope, and workflow live in its own `SKILL.md`. Brand identity specs and channel guidelines live in the skill's `references/` folder.
