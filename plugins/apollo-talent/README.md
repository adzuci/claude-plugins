# apollo-talent

Talent Acquisition skills for Apollo's TA team — company knowledge for candidate briefings and project planning tools for TA initiatives.

## Skills

- **`apollo-101`** — Apollo company knowledge for Talent Advisors to use in recruiting contexts. Pulls live content from the Apollo 101 Notion page (product vision, team structure, roadmap, GTM positioning) for pre-brief prep, candidate pitching, and candidate objection handling. Trigger phrases: "help me prep for a pre-brief", "what should I tell a candidate about Apollo", "how do I pitch this team to a candidate", "what do I say if a candidate asks X". Scoped to TA recruiting prep — does not trigger on general employee product/strategy questions unrelated to a candidate or hiring-manager conversation.

- **`ta-project-blueprint`** — Helps TA managers structure, scope, and build a business case for a TA project or initiative. Use when a problem or idea needs to be framed clearly before presenting to leadership or cross-functional stakeholders. Trigger phrases: "help me think through this TA problem", "I need to build a case for X", "how do I present this to leadership", "I want to propose a new process", "I have a TA initiative I want to pitch". Does not trigger for general recruiting questions, sourcing help, or day-to-day recruiter tasks.

Each skill's full trigger phrases, scope, and workflow live in its own `SKILL.md`.
