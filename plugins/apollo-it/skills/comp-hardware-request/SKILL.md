> **Live reference:** Before responding, fetch the [IT AI Agent Reference](https://www.notion.so/apolloio/352ab2b3b49680d090a4e8aa254b9143) page and use its current **WFH Equipment Stipend** amounts, **Ticket Routing** assignee, and **SLAs**.

Hey! 👋 Before we get into it, it's important to know what IT covers directly vs.
what goes through the WFH Equipment Stipend — they're different paths.

## What does IT handle vs. the stipend?

| Request type | Who handles it |
|---|---|
| **Laptop / MacBook replacement** (broken, performance, refresh) | ✅ IT — open a ticket |
| **Laptop for Finance team** (Windows) | ✅ IT — open a ticket |
| **Peripherals within stipend budget** (monitor, keyboard, mouse, etc.) | 💳 WFH Stipend — buy it yourself and get reimbursed |
| **Peripherals beyond stipend budget** (needs manager approval) | ✅ IT — open a ticket with approval |

______________________________________________________________________

## 💳 Peripherals — WFH Equipment Stipend

Most peripheral requests (monitor, keyboard, mouse, headphones, adapter, laptop stand, webcam, etc.) are covered by the **WFH Equipment Stipend** — you buy the item and get reimbursed.

**Stipend amounts:** Use the current values from the **WFH Equipment Stipend** section of the IT AI Agent Reference page fetched above.

**What the stipend covers:**

- Monitor, desk, ergonomic chair
- Keyboard, mouse, headphones, webcam, microphone
- Laptop stand, docking station, monitor arm, cables, adapters
- Lighting, surge protectors, cable management accessories

**What it does NOT cover:**

- Non-work items or furniture for non-work areas
- Subscriptions with recurring fees
- Anything unrelated to your home office setup

👉 Full details and reimbursement instructions: [WFH Equipment Stipend](https://www.notion.so/apolloio/WFH-Equipment-Stipend-7368ea38b3f049709dde74f62ab8ef0b)

> 💡 **Not sure if something qualifies?** Ask before you buy — I can help you check,
> or you can open a ticket with manager approval and IT will review it.

## 💻 Laptop / Device Replacement — Open an IT ticket

If your laptop needs to be **replaced** (broken, failing hardware, end of life) or you
need a new one, IT handles this directly.

**What you'll need:**

- Business justification (what's wrong, how it's impacting your work)
- Manager approval
- Your region (determines who handles it)

**What to expect:**

- IT will check your device's serial number and warranty status
- If under warranty → sent for repair; IT provides a loaner in the meantime
- If out of warranty or beyond repair → replacement with same specs or better
- ⏱️ **Lead time: 7–10 business days after approval**

> ⚠️ Don't attempt repairs yourself on Apollo-issued hardware — it can void the warranty.

**To open a ticket, tell me:**

1. What device you have (model, approximate age)
1. What's happening (broken screen, won't turn on, overheating, too slow, etc.)
1. Your region (US, LATAM, APAC)
1. Your manager's name

I'll create the ticket in ITSD (with a linked copy in IT Logistics) and assign it to the right person. 🛠️

## 🔧 Troubleshooting first — before requesting a replacement

If your device is slow or acting up, let's try a few things before escalating:

| Issue | Try this first |
|---|---|
| Slow / freezing | Close unused apps, check Activity Monitor (Mac) / Task Manager (Windows), restart |
| Overheating | Clear vents, close heavy apps, check for software updates |
| HDMI / display not working | Try a different cable, restart, check display settings |
| Won't charge | Try a different cable/charger, check for debris in the port |
| Mac locked / "account is locked" | See the Okta Login skill — IT has an automated unlock workflow |

If troubleshooting doesn't help, let me know and I'll escalate to IT.
