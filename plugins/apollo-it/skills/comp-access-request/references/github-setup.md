# GitHub Access — Special Setup

GitHub access requires a mandatory prerequisite before IT can add you to the org.
Skipping it causes automatic account deactivation and alerts Engineering Leadership.

## Step 1 — Add your Apollo email to GitHub

1. Go to **[github.com/settings/emails](https://github.com/settings/emails)**
1. Add your `@apollo.io` email address
1. Verify it via the confirmation email GitHub sends you

> 💡 **Didn't get the verification email?** Check your spam folder, or go back to [github.com/settings/emails](https://github.com/settings/emails) and click **"Resend verification email"** next to your Apollo address.

## Step 2 — Set it as your Public email

1. Go to **[github.com/settings/profile](https://github.com/settings/profile)**
1. Find the **"Public email"** field and select your `@apollo.io` address
1. Scroll down and click **"Update Profile"** ✅

> 💡 **"Public email" field is greyed out?**
> Go to Settings → Emails → uncheck **"Keep my email addresses private"**, then try again.

## Step 3 — Tell IT you're ready

Once both steps above are done, let me know:

- Your **GitHub username**
- Which **repo or team** you need access to (if you know it)
- A quick **reason** (e.g., "engineer on the leadgenie team", "PM collaborating with eng")

I'll open a ticket and IT will send you an invite to the `apolloio` org. 🚀

## What happens next

1. IT sends you a **GitHub organization invitation**
1. You'll get an email and a notification in GitHub — **accept it within 7 days** (it expires!)
1. If you need access to a specific private repo, IT will add you to the right GitHub team

## Common situations

**"I need access to `apolloio/leadgenie`"**
You need org membership first (steps above), then IT will add you to the `leadgenie` team.

**"I got a 404 when trying to open an apolloio repo"**
That means you're not in the org yet. Complete steps 1 & 2 above and let me know.

**"I was in the org but got removed"**
This can happen through our automated audit. As long as you're an active Apollo employee, IT can re-add you. Let me know and I'll open a ticket.

**"The invite expired before I accepted it"**
Just let me know — IT will resend it.
