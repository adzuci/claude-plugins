## Employee advocacy program

Apollo's employee amplification motion combines an official tool and a manual fallback.

### Sprout Social Advocacy (official, capacity-constrained)

- Apollo licenses **Sprout Social Advocacy** as the official employee advocacy platform.
- Seats are **limited and currently full** — new launches cannot rely on adding employees to Sprout Advocacy on demand.
- Treat Sprout coverage as a bonus, not the default distribution path.

### Employee social media guides (default fallback)

- For major launches, the Brand & Social team produces an **employee social media guide** — a doc with suggested posts, suggested visuals, and a "feel free to remix" framing so employees and execs can post personalized versions.
- The guide is shared in three Slack channels:
  - `#apollo-advocacy` — **canonical key channel** for employee amplification
  - `#general` — broad reach
  - `#internal-communications` — official launches and announcements
- `herald:social` should produce this guide as a deliverable (`employee-amplification-guide.md`) for any launch flagged as "major."

## LinkedIn deliverable shape

LinkedIn now has both a brand page and personal/exec posts. Default deliverable for `herald:social` on LinkedIn:

1. **One brand page post** — official announcement from the Apollo brand account. Concise, specific, links to blog post or landing page. Can be longer-form (up to 3000 chars) or short and punchy.
1. **One exec/founder post** (150–300 words) — drafted in the voice of a specific Apollo exec or founder (e.g., CEO, CPO, PM lead). Use first-person, behind-the-build framing where it fits the launch. Hook in the first line (the "see more" line).
1. **Two employee templates** (100–200 words each) — adaptable drafts that any employee can personalize. Each template should:
   - Be obviously remixable (placeholder for personal angle).
   - Carry the launch's core specifics so employees who don't tweak can still post a useful version.
   - Avoid copy-paste-identical-looking phrasing across all employees (vary structure, not just words).

Confirm with the user which exec voice the founder post should use before drafting.
