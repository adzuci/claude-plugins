# Order Form / Upsell Order Form Clause Playbook

Reference for analyzing Order Forms and Upsell Order Forms against Apollo's standard template.

______________________________________________________________________

## 1. Governing Terms Reference

**Standard**: References either the MDSA (with effective date) or Apollo's online Terms
of Service at https://www.apollo.io/terms. Order Form terms govern over Governing Terms
in case of direct conflict, for this Order Form only.

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Governing Terms reference is removed
- Customer's own terms are substituted as governing terms
- No precedence clause clarifying Order Form vs. Governing Terms relationship

______________________________________________________________________

## 2. Contract Term and Start Date

**Standard**: Fixed term in months from Start Date. Clearly stated.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Term is unusually short (< 12 months) without explanation
- Start date is ambiguous or left blank

**🔴 Flag if**:

- No defined term (open-ended agreement)
- Term is made cancelable at Customer's convenience

______________________________________________________________________

## 3. Renewal Terms

**Standard**: Auto-renews unless 30-day written non-renewal notice before end of Term.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Notice period extended to 60 or 90 days (reduces Apollo's renewal window)
- Manual renewal required instead of auto-renew

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Auto-renewal removed entirely with no renewal mechanism
- Customer given unilateral right to terminate without notice at term end

______________________________________________________________________

## 4. Pricing and Fees

**Standard**: Clearly stated annual list price, discount amount/percentage, and net
total per product line (Users, Credits, Advanced Dialer, Inbound, etc.). All fees
non-cancelable and non-refundable.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Discount is unusually large without documented approval
- Fee schedule references external document not attached

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Fees stated as refundable or cancelable during Term
- Price freeze or cap on renewal pricing added
- Most-favored nation (MFN) pricing clause added

______________________________________________________________________

## 5. Payment Terms

**Standard**: Due per payment schedule (annual, semi-annual, quarterly, or monthly as
stated). Net 30 from invoice. USD.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Net 60 or Net 90 payment terms requested
- Payment in non-USD currency

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Payment contingent on conditions (e.g., "upon Customer satisfaction")
- Fees offset against amounts owed by Apollo

______________________________________________________________________

## 6. Product/Service Scope

**Standard**: Defined products with specific user counts, credit amounts, and associated
fees. No implicit expansion of scope.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Vague product descriptions that could imply broader access than intended
- Credits or users described as "unlimited"

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Scope grants access to products/features not reflected in pricing
- Unlimited use or volume commitments from Apollo without caps

______________________________________________________________________

## 7. Addenda

**Standard**: Any addenda (Reseller, Competitor Buyout) are referenced in the Order
Form and attached. Terms of addenda are subordinate to Order Form for this transaction.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Referenced addendum is not attached or not reviewed

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Customer's own addendum or terms are attached and not Apollo's standard form

______________________________________________________________________

## 8. Upsell-Specific: Parent Order Form Reference

*(Upsell Order Forms only)*

**Standard**: Clearly references the parent Order Form date and Ironclad ID. Upsell
term is prorated through the parent Order Form's end date.

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Parent Order Form reference is missing or incorrect
- Upsell creates a separate/independent term not tied to the parent Order Form
- Upsell extends beyond the parent Order Form end date without new Order Form
