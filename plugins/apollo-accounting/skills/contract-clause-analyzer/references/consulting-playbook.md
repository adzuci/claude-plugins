# Consulting Agreement Clause Playbook

Reference for analyzing Consulting Agreements against Apollo's standard template.

______________________________________________________________________

## 1. Services and Statement of Work

**Standard**: Services described in Exhibit A (Statement of Work). Additional SOWs
may be added. Consultant performs only through its own employees and Apollo-approved
subcontractors.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Services are vaguely defined without reference to a SOW
- Subcontractor approval process is removed

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Scope of services is open-ended (no SOW structure)
- Consultant can engage subcontractors without Apollo approval

______________________________________________________________________

## 2. Payment

**Standard**: Fees calculated as set forth in the SOW. Company does not reimburse
expenses unless SOW says otherwise. Expenses are Consultant's sole responsibility.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Broad expense reimbursement added without caps or approval requirements
- Payment schedule is unclear or tied to undefined milestones

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Open-ended or uncapped fee commitments
- Payment made non-contingent on deliverable acceptance

______________________________________________________________________

## 3. Independent Contractor Relationship

**Standard**: Consultant is an independent contractor — not an employee, agent, or
partner. No authority to bind Apollo. Consultant determines manner/means of work.
Must comply with applicable law.

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Employment or agency relationship language is inserted
- Consultant is given authority to bind Apollo
- Control over manner and means of work is given to Apollo (creates misclassification risk)

______________________________________________________________________

## 4. Taxes and Benefits

**Standard**: Consultant responsible for all taxes, withholding, social security,
workers' comp, etc. Consultant Personnel not entitled to any Apollo employee benefits.
Consultant indemnifies Apollo for any tax obligations imposed by law.

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Apollo assumes tax withholding obligations
- Consultant Personnel receive any benefit entitlements
- Indemnification for tax obligations is removed

______________________________________________________________________

## 5. IP Ownership and Assignment

**Standard**: All work product, inventions, developments, and deliverables created by
Consultant in connection with the Services are "work made for hire" or, if not, are
assigned to Apollo. Consultant assigns all rights, including moral rights.

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Work-for-hire or assignment language is absent
- Consultant retains ownership or license rights to deliverables
- "Background IP" carve-out is overly broad (captures work product Apollo expected to own)

______________________________________________________________________

## 6. Confidentiality

**Standard**: Consultant treats all Apollo Confidential Information as confidential.
Obligations survive termination.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Confidentiality period is limited (e.g., 1 year) rather than indefinite for trade secrets

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Confidentiality obligations are absent
- Consultant can use Apollo's confidential information for its own business

______________________________________________________________________

## 7. Non-Solicitation / Non-Compete

**Standard**: Apollo's standard template typically includes restrictions on Consultant
soliciting Apollo employees during the term and for a period after.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Non-solicitation is absent (flag for awareness)

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Reverse non-solicitation is inserted (prevents Apollo from hiring counterparty's staff)

______________________________________________________________________

## 8. Indemnification

**Standard**: Consultant indemnifies Apollo for: Consultant's negligence or willful
misconduct, IP infringement by Consultant's work, Consultant's tax obligations,
violations of law by Consultant.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Indemnification scope is narrowed to willful misconduct only (excludes negligence)

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Indemnification is reversed — Apollo indemnifies Consultant with no reciprocity
- Consultant's IP infringement indemnity is removed

______________________________________________________________________

## 9. Limitation of Liability

**Standard**: Apollo's standard Consulting Agreement typically mirrors the MDSA
cap structure: no consequential damages; direct damages capped at fees paid under
the Agreement in the prior 12 months.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Cap is tied to total contract value rather than 12-month fees (inflates cap for
  long-term agreements)

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Consequential damages exclusion is removed
- Cap is removed entirely

______________________________________________________________________

## 10. Term and Termination

**Standard**: Term as stated in SOW. Either party may terminate for cause with
30-day notice and cure period. Company may terminate for convenience with reasonable
notice.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Termination for convenience requires payment of remaining fees
- Cure period is extended beyond 30 days

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Apollo cannot terminate for convenience
- Consultant has termination rights that could disrupt ongoing work without replacement
