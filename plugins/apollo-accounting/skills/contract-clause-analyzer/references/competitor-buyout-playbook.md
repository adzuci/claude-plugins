# Competitor Contract Buyout Addendum Clause Playbook

Reference for analyzing Competitor Contract Buyout Addenda against Apollo's standard template.

______________________________________________________________________

## 1. Fee Waiver Period and Amount

**Standard**: Defined fee waiver period (in months from Start Date). Subscription Fees
waived in full during this period. Customer has full access to Services during the
Fee Waiver Period. Waived amount clearly stated.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Fee waiver period is unusually long (> 6 months) without deal approval
- Waived amount is not clearly stated

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Fee waiver extends beyond the Initial Term
- Customer's access during Fee Waiver Period is reduced compared to standard service

______________________________________________________________________

## 2. Minimum Subscription Term

**Standard**: Customer agrees to a minimum subscription term (in months from Effective
Date). The Initial Term includes the Fee Waiver Period — waiver period is not additive
to the contract term.

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Fee Waiver Period is additive to the contract term (inflates total commitment)
- No minimum subscription term stated
- Minimum term is shorter than the fee waiver period

______________________________________________________________________

## 3. Pricing Structure

**Standard**: Three-tier pricing: (i) $0 during Fee Waiver Period; (ii) stated fee
from end of waiver to end of first 12 months; (iii) annual fee from month 13 through
end of Contract Term. All amounts clearly specified.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Year 2+ pricing is not specified (creates ambiguity at renewal)
- Pricing structure deviates from the three-tier format without deal approval

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Pricing structure makes fees contingent on performance or usage thresholds
- Any tier is stated as "TBD" or left blank

______________________________________________________________________

## 4. Early Termination

**Standard**: If Customer terminates before end of Initial Term for any reason other
than Apollo's material breach, Customer immediately pays: (i) all waived Subscription
Fees for the Fee Waiver Period; AND (ii) all remaining Subscription Fees through end
of Initial Term.

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Early termination penalty is removed or reduced
- Customer can terminate without repaying waived fees
- "Material breach" exception is broadened (e.g., includes any Apollo service issue)
- Cure period is not referenced (Customer should not be able to terminate before cure
  period expires under the main Agreement)

______________________________________________________________________

## 5. Customer Representations and Warranties

**Standard**: Customer warrants that: (i) it has reviewed existing contractual
obligations with current providers; (ii) entering this Agreement does not violate
any existing obligation; (iii) use of Services will not breach any third-party
agreement; (iv) Apollo has not encouraged Customer to breach any existing agreement;
(v) all information provided about existing provider contracts is true and complete.

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Any of these representations are removed
- Customer does not represent that Apollo has not induced a breach (exposes Apollo
  to tortious interference claims)

______________________________________________________________________

## 6. Non-Transferability of Program Benefits

**Standard**: Buyout benefits are non-transferable unless Apollo explicitly permits
in writing.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Transferability is made automatic on Customer's assignment of the Agreement

______________________________________________________________________

## 7. Marketing Rights

**Standard**: Customer agrees Apollo may disclose Customer as a client in marketing
materials after the Fee Waiver Period ends.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Marketing rights are removed or Customer retains veto rights over all marketing use
