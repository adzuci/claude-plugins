# Reseller Addendum Clause Playbook

Reference for analyzing Reseller Addenda against Apollo's standard template.

______________________________________________________________________

## 1. End User Definition

**Standard**: End User is a user/recipient of PGI under a valid Customer Agreement who:
(i) is not a direct competitor of Apollo; (ii) uses PGI solely for internal business
purposes; (iii) is subject to restrictions at least as protective as the MDSA.

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Competitor exclusion is removed
- End User allowed to use PGI for external/commercial redistribution
- End User restrictions are less protective than Apollo's MDSA restrictions

______________________________________________________________________

## 2. Reseller License Scope

**Standard**: Limited license to resell/sublicense PGI obtained from the Apollo Platform
during the applicable Order Form term. Customer must enter a Customer Agreement with
each End User. Further resale or sublicensing by End Users is explicitly prohibited.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Customer Agreement template is not subject to Apollo review on request
- Further sublicensing restriction is not explicit

**🔴 Flag if**:

- License extends beyond PGI to the Contributor Database or Platform itself
- Term of reseller license extends beyond the applicable Order Form term
- End Users are permitted to further resell or sublicense

______________________________________________________________________

## 3. Customer Agreement Requirements

**Standard**: Customer Agreement with each End User must contain restrictions
substantively equivalent to the MDSA, including all Section 3 restrictions. Must
prohibit further resale/sublicensing. Apollo may request a copy of the template.

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Customer Agreement restrictions are weaker than the MDSA
- Apollo's right to review the Customer Agreement template is removed
- Further resale prohibition is absent from Customer Agreement requirements

______________________________________________________________________

## 4. Apollo Branding

**Standard**: Customer must integrate Apollo-approved branding in any End User-facing
portions of its platform that display PGI.

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Branding requirement is removed or made optional

______________________________________________________________________

## 5. PGI Storage and Caching

**Standard**: Customer may cache/store PGI only on a per-End User basis, only as
necessary to support functionality for that specific End User, securely associated
with and accessible only by that End User. No centralized/shared/global cache or
system-wide storage accessible to multiple End Users.

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Centralized storage of PGI is permitted
- PGI accessible to multiple End Users from a shared cache
- Storage restrictions are removed or significantly weakened

______________________________________________________________________

## 6. License Restrictions

**Standard**: Customer must not: use PGI to build competing products; use Services/data
to create/train/improve competing products; use PGI to solicit Apollo's customers;
use access to analyze Apollo's technology for competitive purposes.

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Any of the above restrictions are removed
- Competitive use prohibition is narrowed to only direct competition
- "Solicit Apollo's customers" restriction is absent

______________________________________________________________________

## 7. Customer Liability for End Users

**Standard**: Customer is liable to Apollo for End Users' acts and omissions as if
they were Customer's own.

**🔴 Flag if**:

- Customer liability for End User conduct is removed or capped
- Customer liability is limited to "commercially reasonable efforts" to control End Users

______________________________________________________________________

## 8. Compliance Documentation

**Standard**: Customer must provide Apollo with reasonable documentation demonstrating
compliance upon request (with reasonable redactions for third-party confidentiality).

**🟡 Flag if**:

- Audit/documentation right is removed
