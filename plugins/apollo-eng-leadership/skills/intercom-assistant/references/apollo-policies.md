# Apollo Policies Reference

Use this reference when a customer asks about eligibility, age, legal, compliance, ToS, privacy, or account policy questions. Check here before asserting any policy fact, and link to the authoritative document in customer-facing replies.

## Terms of Service

**URL:** https://www.apollo.io/terms

Key provisions relevant to support:

- **Age eligibility:** "All Authorized Users must be of legal age and comply with these Terms." There is no parental consent exception in the published Terms. This is the full answer for any question about whether minors can use Apollo.
- **Authorized Users:** Defined as employees or service providers of the subscribing organization who have been expressly authorized by the account holder. Only the account holder can authorize users.
- **Acceptable use:** Apollo is licensed for internal business use only. Use to power external products, share data with customers, or resell Apollo data is not permitted under standard terms.
- **Credit expiry:** Credits expire at end of billing cycle (paid) or end of monthly term (unpaid). Credits do not roll over.
- **Modifications:** Apollo may change features, pricing, and credit conversion policies at any time with notification.

When citing ToS to a customer, link directly to https://www.apollo.io/terms and name the relevant section (e.g. "Authorized Users section").

## Privacy Policy

**URL:** https://www.apollo.io/privacy-policy

Use for questions about data collection, data removal requests, GDPR/CCPA rights, or what Apollo stores about a user or contact.

- Data removal requests: direct customers to https://www.apollo.io/privacy-policy/remove or the Privacy Center at https://www.apollo.io/company/privacy-center.

## When to Use This Reference

- Customer asks about age requirements, eligibility, or whether a specific type of user can access Apollo
- Customer asks about data rights, removal, or privacy obligations
- Customer asks what Apollo is or is not allowed to be used for
- Customer references "your terms" or "your policy" without specifying which one
- Any question where the answer should be grounded in a published legal document rather than a KB article

## What Not to Assert Without Checking

- Do not state a specific minimum age number (e.g. "you must be 18") — the ToS says "legal age" which varies by jurisdiction. Reference the ToS and let it speak for itself.
- Do not invent exceptions or carve-outs that are not in the published documents.
- If the question goes beyond the published ToS (e.g. internal legal interpretation, enterprise contract terms), escalate via `#ama-support-peer-assist` and note that Legal/Privacy team confirmation is needed.
