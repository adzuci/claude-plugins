# Text Blaze Snippets

Text Blaze is a Chrome extension that expands short keyboard shortcuts into full reply text. Unlike Intercom personal macros, it works across any browser tab — useful for composing replies in chat, email, or notes outside Intercom.

**Limitation**: Text Blaze cannot dynamically insert the customer's first name the way Intercom's `{{First name}}` substitution can. Use Intercom macros when first-name personalization is needed.

## Installation

1. Open the Chrome Web Store and search **Text Blaze**, or go directly to the Text Blaze listing.
1. Click **Add to Chrome** → **Add extension**.
1. Sign in or create a free account at `blaze.today`.
1. Open `dashboard.blaze.today` to manage snippets.
1. Create a folder (e.g. **Support Rotation**) to keep rotation snippets organized.
1. Add each snippet: click **+** (top-left) → enter a **Label** and a **Shortcut** (e.g. `/hello`) → paste the body text → **Save**.

## Wave 2 Snippets

Five snippets from the Wave 2 rotation (shared by Adam Blackwell after shadowing Claudia Espinosa, 2026-06-24). When presenting these during `setup`, substitute the EM's actual name for `[Name]` — the goal is ready-to-paste bodies, not a template to edit later.

| Shortcut | Label | When to use | Body |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/hello` | Hi + Call | Default first reply — customer has raised an issue and you're reviewing it | Hi! Thanks for reaching out. I'm [Name], one of our Product Advocates. I'm reviewing your issue now, if it's easier, I'm happy to jump on a quick call and work through it with you. |
| `/higoal` | Hi + Goal + Call | First reply when the customer has a goal or question and you want to invite elaboration | Hi there, I'm [Name], a Product Advocate here to help. Could you share a bit more about your goal, or if it's easier, I'm happy to jump on a quick call and work through it with you. |
| `/intro` | Intro | Simple intro when a longer greeting is not needed | Hi, thank you for reaching out. I am [Name] from our Product Advocate team and am happy to assist you. |
| `/bye` | Bye | Closing after the customer confirms satisfied | You're welcome! Really glad we could get that sorted for you. I'd appreciate it if you're able to rate your experience and hope you have a great rest of your day! |
| `/nudge` | Nudge | On a call when you can't hear the customer | Hi there! Just checking if our connection is still working. Is everything okay on your end? Let me know if you need any assistance. Looking forward to staying connected! |
