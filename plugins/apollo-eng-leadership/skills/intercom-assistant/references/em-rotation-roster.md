## Use In Escalations

- Treat a caller as a verified EM only when an exact roster-name match is corroborated by trusted, authenticated identity metadata, such as the caller's signed-in profile or authenticated user record. A name match alone is not sufficient. Only then offer a reviewed, paste-ready `#ama-technical-support` Apollo Operator ask when the technical Slack path is warranted.
- Resolve trusted identity from the host's signed-in profile or a Slack MCP user-profile lookup. If neither is available, provide the reviewed manual-channel draft and link, but do not send an EM-channel Slack MCP message.
- A Product Advocate or Customer Advocate may take a reviewed Apollo Operator research question to `#ama-pa-apollo-operator`. If the customer needs a technical handoff, prepare an Intercom internal-note draft and propose the confirmed Technical Support workflow for a human to carry out.
- Do not infer EM status from job title, team name, a stated display name, or a past support conversation. If authenticated identity metadata is unavailable, incomplete, or ambiguous, ask one concise question and do not offer any Slack route.

## Maintenance

- Refresh this reference from the rotation schedule before each new wave and at least every eight weeks during an active wave. The planning sheet contains additional EMs without a verified wave assignment, so it is intentionally not used as a Slack-access allowlist.
- Keep a prior EM on the technical-queue path when their name is absent or their wave assignment cannot be verified. This is the safe default for roster drift, name changes, and new participants.
