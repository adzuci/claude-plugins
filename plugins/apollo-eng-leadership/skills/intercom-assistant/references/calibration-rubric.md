# Calibration Rubric

Use this reference for `calibration` when the user provides a recording link, Granola link, transcript file, or pasted transcript.

Default rubric source:
`https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1qCnFqsIgQr4XfvAP8wGwqjT5d_lQ1YGiWv0BiAYZVI4/edit?gid=0#gid=0`

## Rules

- Base feedback only on the transcript/notes and provided metadata.
- Do not assume intent.
- Do not invent missing information.
- If evidence is unavailable, say `Not enough information`.
- Quote short evidence snippets or provide accurate transcript-grounded summaries.
- This is reference feedback only. Final scoring and performance decisions belong to human reviewers.
- Score a step or dimension only if the call context required it. A step that did not apply (for example, no call offer on a true one-line answer, or no Discovery on a 30-second fact lookup) is `N/A`, not a deduction. The `pre-call-check.md` 7-step framework (Welcome, Verify, Set Expectations, Clarify and Align, Discovery, Troubleshoot and Guide, Confirm and Close) gives "Call Framing and Expectation Setting" and "Outcome Standard Met" a shared vocabulary; treat it as a checklist of applicable steps, not a mandatory all-seven sequence.

## Live Call Dimensions

Evaluate with `Good`, `Mixed`, or `Poor` anchors:

- Urgency & Immediate Action: forward progress and momentum.
- Full Ownership: accountability, leadership, and clear ownership through escalation if needed.
- High-Bandwidth Guidance: clear, structured, confident live communication.
- Customer Understanding: whether the live environment improved the customer's understanding.
- Investigation & Technical Guidance: diagnostic quality and accurate guidance.
- Handling Complex Problems: professional uncertainty management without speculation.
- Call Framing & Expectation Setting: clear goal, success definition, and expectation resets.
- Outcome Standard Met: meaningful progress, clear next steps, and ownership.

Also include:

- CX impact: customer effort, confidence/trust, clarity of next steps, emotional state when supported by evidence.
- Reliability & internal hygiene: taxonomy, account actions, internal notes, and process adherence when evidence exists.
- Calibration decision: acceptable as-is, coaching opportunity, process/system issue, or requires immediate follow-up.
