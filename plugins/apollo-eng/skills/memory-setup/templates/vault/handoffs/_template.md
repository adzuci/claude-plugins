# Oncall Handoff

## Open Alerts

Firing or escalating alerts, expected to fire, or alerts to watch.

## Risky Recent Changes

Deployments, config changes, or maintenance in the last 48 hours that may contribute to incidents.

## Partially-Mitigated Issues

Known problems with temporary fixes, waiting on permanent resolution.

## Customer-Facing Threads

Slack channels, support tickets, or escalations discussing platform issues.

## Tickets Near MTTA/MTTR

SLO deadlines approaching on open incidents or production bugs.
