# Caller Interview

Ask at least five concise questions in one batch before the final assessment. Use the caller's existing context, Glean findings, and public evidence to avoid asking for facts already known. Replace an answered question with another material gap.

Prioritize these questions:

1. What business problem should this solve, and what would a useful outcome look like?
1. Who is the accountable business owner and which team would operate it?
1. Who would use it, how many people are in the pilot, and which repos or workflows are in scope?
1. What Apollo, customer, employee, source-code, credential, or meeting data could it touch?
1. Which delivery mode will be used: local CLI, OSS library, hosted SaaS, browser extension, MCP, or a combination?
1. What permissions, integrations, network destinations, and third parties would the proposed setup enable?
1. Is there an existing Zip, Security, or Legal request, contract, approval, or known exception to link?
1. What pilot stages, success criteria, stop conditions, and cleanup steps are proposed?

When the caller does not know an answer, record it as a gap instead of suggesting a safe-sounding value. Keep the interview collaborative: explain that the answers make the Zip and review handoff faster.
