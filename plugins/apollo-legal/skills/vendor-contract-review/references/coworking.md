# Co-working Space Contract Review Reference

Use this file when triaging a contract classified as a **Co-working Space Contract** (co-working membership, hot desk, dedicated desk, private office, shared workspace, facility access agreement, day pass).

---

## Scope Note

Co-working space contracts are reviewed based purely on terms — there is no dollar threshold that makes a contract automatically green or exempt. Even low-cost memberships get scanned because the primary risks are in non-financial clauses (IP claims, data/system access) rather than spend level.

These contracts will almost always be GREEN. The scan is a fast check for two specific hard stops. Most co-working space contracts don't contain either issue.

---

## Clause Review Matrix

### IP Rights on Work Created at Space
- **Desired**: Explicit statement that the facility claims no rights over any IP, work product, or inventions created by members at the facility.
- **Acceptable if absent**: No IP clause at all. Silence on IP is fine — the concern is only if the facility affirmatively claims rights. Do not flag absence.
- **Will NOT Accept**: Any clause claiming facility ownership of, or a license to, work product, inventions, or materials created at or in connection with the space by Apollo or its employees.

### Data Security & System Access
- **Desired**: No requirement for the facility to access Apollo's systems, internal networks, employee credentials, or business data as a condition of membership.
- **Fallback (acceptable)**: Limited access for billing and membership administration only (e.g., billing portal login), with explicit scope limits and no access to Apollo's internal systems.
- **Will NOT Accept**: Facility requires access to Apollo's internal systems, corporate networks, employee personal data beyond what's needed for billing, or any data classified as Apollo confidential information.

### Indemnification
- **Desired**: Standard one-sided facility indemnification — Apollo (as member) indemnifies the facility for claims arising from Apollo's own use of the space. This is normal and acceptable market practice. **Rate this GREEN.**
- **Fallback**: Standard one-sided is the expected norm. No fallback needed — it's already the preferred position.
- **Will NOT Accept**: Apollo indemnifies the facility for the facility's own negligence, misconduct, or failure to maintain the premises safely. The indemnification should always carve out the facility's own fault.

### Term & Auto-Renewal
- **Desired**: Month-to-month with no auto-renewal, or termination on 30-day or less written notice.
- **Fallback (acceptable)**: Auto-renewal is acceptable if Apollo can terminate with 31 to 60 days written notice.
- **Will NOT Accept**: Annual commitment with no termination for convenience right, or auto-renewal with required notice period greater than 60 days.

### Liability Cap
- **Desired**: Apollo's liability capped at the monthly membership fee.
- **Fallback (acceptable)**: Apollo's liability capped at 3 months of membership fees.
- **Will NOT Accept**: No liability cap at all — unlimited financial exposure for Apollo in connection with use of the space.

### Governing Law & Dispute Resolution
- **Desired**: Standard commercial jurisdiction (California, Delaware, or the state where the facility is located). Litigation preferred.
- **Fallback (acceptable)**: Any well-established commercial jurisdiction. Arbitration acceptable under standard rules (AAA or JAMS) with reasonable venue.
- **Will NOT Accept**: Mandatory arbitration in an unusual jurisdiction, or governing law in a jurisdiction with significantly unfavorable commercial law for members.

## Green / Yellow / Red Assessment Rules

### GREEN — all of the following are true:
- No IP claims on work created at the space (or IP clause is absent — absence is fine)
- No access to Apollo systems, data, or networks required (beyond billing)
- Standard one-sided facility indemnification (acceptable, not a flag)
- Month-to-month or auto-renewal with ≤30-day termination notice
- No access to Apollo employee or customer personal data beyond standard membership registration (name, email, billing info)

### YELLOW — one or more of the following, but no RED triggers:
- Auto-renewal with termination notice of 31–60 days (flag the auto-renewal clause; likely acceptable with a calendar reminder in place)
- Annual financial commitment with termination for convenience (flag the term — acceptable if Apollo retains an exit right, but the reviewer should confirm the commitment level is appropriate)
- Indemnification goes beyond standard venue protection in a potentially material way (flag the specific language)
- No liability cap at all (flag for a quick look, especially if it's not a month-to-month membership)

### RED — any one of these is a hard stop:
- Any IP claim on work product, inventions, or materials created at the space
- Facility requires access to Apollo's internal systems, corporate network, or employee data beyond billing
- Facility requires access to Apollo employee or customer personal data beyond standard membership registration (name, email, billing) — e.g., corporate directory, HR records, or any data classified as Apollo confidential information
- Annual commitment with no termination for convenience right
- Apollo indemnifies the facility for the facility's own negligence or misconduct
