# Marketing Contract Review Reference

Use this file when triaging a contract classified as a **Marketing Contract** (community partner agreement, case study, participant agreement, participant release, co-marketing, brand collaboration, testimonial, joint content, social media agreement, etc.).

---

## Apollo Template Shortcut

Apollo maintains three standard templates for marketing agreements:
- Community Partner Agreement
- Participant Agreement (Case Study)
- Participant Release (Case Study)

**If the contract is one of these templates returned with zero changes** (only blanks filled in, no substantive modifications) → **rate GREEN immediately** without running the full checklist. The templates are pre-approved.

If the contract is based on an Apollo template but has been modified, continue with the checklist below and focus on what changed.

## Clause Review Matrix

### IP / Content Ownership
- **Desired**: Apollo retains all IP in its own brand, content, and materials. The partner grants Apollo a limited, non-exclusive license to use their name, logo, likeness, and story for the specific marketing purpose. Apollo grants a reciprocal limited license for co-branded use only.
- **Fallback (acceptable)**: Joint ownership of co-created content is acceptable if Apollo has full, unrestricted commercial use rights with no consent requirement from the partner.
- **Will NOT Accept**: Partner owns Apollo-created content; partner retains a perpetual or irrevocable license to Apollo's brand or materials; or partner has the right to sublicense Apollo's content to third parties.

### Approval Rights
- **Desired**: Apollo has final approval over all content featuring Apollo's brand, name, employees, or likeness before publication. Review window: 5 business days.
- **Fallback (acceptable)**: Review window extended to 10 business days. Silence-equals-approval after adequate notice is acceptable if the window is sufficient (7+ days).
- **Will NOT Accept**: No approval right for Apollo — partner can publish content featuring Apollo without Apollo's prior consent.

### Indemnification
- **Desired**: Mutual indemnification. Each party indemnifies the other for claims arising from its own content, IP infringement, or breach of the agreement.
- **Fallback (acceptable)**: Mutual indemnification with a narrowed scope (limited to IP infringement and gross negligence / willful misconduct).
- **Will NOT Accept**: Apollo indemnifies the partner for the partner's own content, actions, or IP infringement. One-sided indemnification flowing only against Apollo.

### License Grants (to Apollo)
- **Desired**: Limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license for the specific marketing purpose described in the agreement. License ends when the agreement ends.
- **Fallback (acceptable)**: Limited license with a reasonable fixed term (up to 2 years), with option to renew.
- **Will NOT Accept**: Perpetual, irrevocable, or freely transferable/sublicensable license to the partner's brand, likeness, or content.

### Confidentiality
- **Desired**: Mutual confidentiality. Both parties protect each other's non-public information.
- **Fallback (acceptable)**: One-way confidentiality protecting Apollo's information is acceptable when the relationship is primarily outbound (e.g., Apollo is the only party sharing non-public details).
- **Will NOT Accept**: No confidentiality provisions at all in a co-marketing or case study agreement where Apollo may share non-public information (e.g., unreleased product details, internal data).

### Term & Termination
- **Desired**: 1 year or less. Apollo has termination for convenience on 30-day written notice. Content already published under a valid license survives termination.
- **Fallback (acceptable)**: Term up to 2 years with termination for convenience on 60-day notice.
- **Will NOT Accept**: No termination right for Apollo, or auto-renewal with no notice period or an unreasonably short window (under 30 days).

## Green / Yellow / Red Assessment Rules

### GREEN — any of the following:
- Apollo's template used with **zero changes** (blanks filled in only) → auto-GREEN
- Minor administrative or process changes only (approval timelines, notice periods, contact info, format) → GREEN
- Minor substantive changes that don't materially shift Apollo's position (e.g., IP scope adjusted slightly, approval period extended to 7 days, small rewording of standard obligations)

### YELLOW — one or more of the following, but no RED triggers:
- Structural changes to IP scope or approval rights that could affect Apollo's position, but the change is minor and doesn't transfer ownership (flag the specific clause)
- Approval period extended beyond 10 business days, or very short silence-equals-approval window
- Indemnification scope broadened but still mutual (e.g., data breach trigger added)
- Term extended beyond 2 years or auto-renewal with notice period under 30 days
- Confidentiality missing in a context where Apollo shares non-public information

### RED — any one of these is a hard stop:
- Structural IP changes that could give the partner ownership of or broad irrevocable rights over Apollo's brand, content, or materials
- No approval right for Apollo over content featuring Apollo's brand or employees
- Apollo indemnifies partner for partner's own content or actions (one-sided indemnification against Apollo)
- Perpetual, irrevocable, or sublicensable license granted to Apollo's brand or content
- Partner will access or process Apollo customer data, employee PII, or any personal data beyond what is needed to produce the agreed marketing content (participant names, quotes, and images of willing participants are expected and acceptable) — requires DPA and full Cat 1 review
