## FY27 Quarter Boundaries

Apollo uses a **February-start fiscal year**. FY27 starts Feb 1, 2026.

| Quarter | Start | End | Notes |
|---------|-------|-----|-------|
| **FY27 Q1** | Feb 1, 2026 | Apr 30, 2026 | Current quarter (as of Apr 3) |
| **FY27 Q2** | May 1, 2026 | Jul 31, 2026 | |
| **FY27 Q3** | Aug 1, 2026 | Oct 31, 2026 | |
| **FY27 Q4** | Nov 1, 2026 | Jan 31, 2027 | |

## Fiscal Year Naming

- **FY27** = Feb 2026 – Jan 2027
- **FY26** = Feb 2025 – Jan 2026
- **FY28** = Feb 2027 – Jan 2028

The "FY" number is the calendar year of the fiscal start + 1 (e.g., fiscal year starting Feb 2026 = FY27).

## When Someone Says...

| They say | They mean |
|----------|-----------|
| "this quarter" (in Apr 2026) | FY27 Q1 (Feb–Apr 2026) — Q1 hasn't closed yet |
| "last quarter" (in Apr 2026) | FY26 Q4 (Nov 2025–Jan 2026) |
| "Q1 close" | April 30 |
| "Q2 planning" | Planning for May–Jul deliverables |
| "end of year" / "FY27 target" | Jan 31, 2027 |
| "FY26 baseline" | FY26 actuals (Feb 2025–Jan 2026) |
