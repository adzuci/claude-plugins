# Business Glossary

Business term definitions with SQL predicates where applicable. Maps to `LU_BUSINESS_GLOSSARY` in Snowflake.

**Status:** 47 terms. Generated 2026-03-31 from Snowflake.

| Term | Definition | SQL Predicate | Related Tables | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ARR | Annual Recurring Revenue. MRR * 12. The primary topline metric. | `ARR column directly` | FCT_TEAM_REVENUE_DAILY, FCT_DAILY_REVENUE, FCT_MONTHLY_REVENUE, DIM_SALESFORCE_APOLLO_TEAMS | Finance / Analytics |
| Account Segment | Tiered classification of SF accounts: Enterprise > Mid-Market > SMB > VSB. Central to all executive reporting. | `ACCOUNT_SEGMENT IN ('Enterprise', 'Mid-Market', 'SMB', 'VSB')` | DIM_SALESFORCE_ACCOUNTS, LU_TEAM_ATTRIBUTES | Sales Ops |
| Account Segment Override | Manual reclassification of account segment. Triggers on closed-won ARR >= $50k (SMB to MM). Locked intra-year after assignment. | `ACCOUNT_SEGMENT_OVERRIDE column` | DIM_SALESFORCE_ACCOUNTS, LU_TEAM_ATTRIBUTES | Sales Ops |
| Active Days | Count of distinct days per month with credit usage > 0 for a team. #1 retention signal: 26+ days = 76% retention vs <40% at 1-5 days. | `Derived: COUNT(DISTINCT ds) WHERE credits_used > 0` | AGG_TEAM_CREDITS, FCT_TEAM_REVENUE_DAILY | Analytics (Brighid) |
| Active User | A user who performed at least one qualifying action on a given day. Canonical definition from Data Science (INT_ACTIVE_USERS_DAILY). Qualifying actions (15 total): outreach automatic email, outreach manual email, extension email, win/close deal, API enrichment, CRM living data enrichment, CSV enrichment, waterfall enrichment, traditional genpipe, nextgen DIY genpipe, meeting assistant, scheduler, notes, CRM record management, browser extension used. Active windows: L1 (today), L7 (last 7 days), L28 (last 28 days). | `IS_ACTIVE_L1 = 1 / IS_ACTIVE_L7 = 1 / IS_ACTIVE_L28 = 1` | INT_ACTIVE_USERS_DAILY, DIM_ACTIVE_USERS_DAILY, PRODUCT_METRICS_ACTIVE | Data Science |
| Change Category | Revenue movement type for a team in a period. Values: new, new_reactivated, reactivation, upgrade, downgrade, renewed, churn, null. | `CHANGE_CATEGORY column` | FCT_MONTHLY_REVENUE, FCT_DAILY_REVENUE | Analytics |
| Core Account | All paid Apollo teams — any team with ARR > $0. Core is a business segment classification, not a free-email filter. Do NOT use IS_CORE_ACCOUNT + HAS_FREE_EMAIL_DOMAIN — this is not the canonical predicate. Free-email exclusion (IS_FREE_EMAIL_DOMAIN_IND = 0) is a separate attribute unrelated to the Core segment definition. | `IS_CORE = TRUE (LU_TEAM_ATTRIBUTES) or ARR > 0` | LU_TEAM_ATTRIBUTES, DIM_SALESFORCE_ACCOUNTS | Analytics |
| Credit Type | Category of credit allocation: email_credit, export_credit, direct_dial_credit, unified_lead_credit, ai_email, ai_credit, power_up_credit, form_enrichment_credit, website_visitor_credit, inbound_website_visitor_credit, conversation_credit, contact_website_visitor_credit, web_search_record_credit. | `CREDIT_TYPE column` | FCT_TEAM_CREDIT_LIMITS_DAILY, FCT_TEAM_CREDIT_USE_DAILY, AGG_TEAM_CREDITS | Monetization |
| Credit Utilization Rate | Credits used divided by credit limit for a given credit type and period. Key signal for pricing and packaging — high utilization drives upgrade conversation. | `DIV0(SUM(credits_used), NULLIF(SUM(credit_limit), 0))` | FCT_TEAM_CREDIT_USE_DAILY, FCT_TEAM_CREDIT_LIMITS_DAILY, FCT_TEAM_CREDITS_DAILY | Analytics / Monetization |
| DAU | Daily Active Users. Count of users with IS_ACTIVE_L1 = 1 on a given day — i.e., performed at least one qualifying action that day. See Active User for qualifying action list. | `COUNT(*) WHERE IS_ACTIVE_L1 = 1` | INT_ACTIVE_USERS_DAILY, PRODUCT_METRICS_ACTIVE | Data Science |
| DCS | Digital Customer Success. Focuses on VSB/SMB retention through automated lifecycle programs. Targets 5% churn reduction. |  | FCT_MONTHLY_REVENUE, DIM_SALESFORCE_ACCOUNTS | CS |
| DORA Metrics | DevOps Research and Assessment metrics: deploy frequency, lead time, MTTR, change failure rate. | `Derived from FCT_GITHUB_PR_TIMELINE events` | FCT_GITHUB_PR_TIMELINE, FCT_LEADGENIE_GITHUB_COMMIT_DATA | Engineering |
| F14D Habit RA Rate | First-14-day Habit Record Actioned rate. Onboarding & Activation KR metric. Team performed 4+ Record Actions within 14 days of signup. Golden Population (SMB+) current: ~12.2%, target: 17%. | `Derived: COUNT of record_actioned events >= 4 within 14 days of TEAM_CREATED_DATE` | DIM_ACTIVE_TEAMS_DAILY, FCT_TEAM_ACTIVITY_DAILY | O&A (Adhiraj) |
| F28D Multi-Product Attach | Percent of paid WAT teams activating 2+ use cases within first 28 days. Baseline: ~31.5-35%. Distinct from ongoing use cases per Paid WAT. | `Derived: COUNT of active use cases >= 2 within 28 days of first active date` | DIM_ACTIVE_TEAMS_DAILY, TEAM_DAILY_SOLUTION_USAGE | Product Analytics |
| Feature Type | Category of Apollo feature that consumed credits. ai_email is commonly excluded from credit totals. | `FEATURE_TYPE column` | AGG_TEAM_CREDITS | Monetization |
| Fiscal Year | Apollo fiscal year starts in February. FY2027-Q1 = Feb 2026. Format: FY2027-1. No shared fiscal calendar dimension table exists yet. | `YEAR_QUARTER column` | FCT_ACCOUNT_QUARTERLY_NRR, LU_FISCAL_CALENDAR | Finance |
| GRR | Gross Revenue Retention. Starting ARR minus churn and downgrade, divided by Starting ARR. No expansion credit. | `(PREVIOUS_DATE_PERIOD_ARR + churn_arr + downgrade_arr) / PREVIOUS_DATE_PERIOD_ARR` | FCT_MONTHLY_REVENUE | Analytics |
| GTME | Go-To-Market Engineer. Account owner / CSM equivalent. Tracks ramping status. | `GTME_NAME, IS_GTME_RAMPING` | DIM_SALESFORCE_ACCOUNTS | CS / Sales |
| Golden Population | SMB + Mid-Market + Enterprise teams in AMER or EMEA (excluding UAE & India). Freemail teams = Gen Pop regardless of employee size. SMB+ = Employee Size >= 20. Default population for onboarding/activation OKRs. | `ACCOUNT_SEGMENT IN ('SMB','Mid-Market','Enterprise') AND ACCOUNT_REGION IN ('AMER','EMEA') AND IS_FREE_EMAIL_DOMAIN_IND = 0` | DIM_SALESFORCE_ACCOUNTS, DIM_ACTIVE_TEAMS_DAILY, LU_TEAM_ATTRIBUTES | Growth / O&A |
| IBU | Individual Business Unit. A paid team with exactly 1 seat. Tracked at team level (PIBU__C) and rolled up to account level. Volatile — recalculated when seat count changes. | `TOTAL_ACTIVE_ARR > 0 AND TOTAL_SEATS = 1` | DIM_SALESFORCE_ACCOUNTS, FCT_DAILY_REVENUE | Sales Ops |
| Inbound Revenue Attribution | Revenue attributed to inbound marketing channels. OKR target: $4M/$10M. Attribution model TBD — requires Marketing Analytics definition. |  | DIM_SALESFORCE_OPPORTUNITIES, FCT_MONTHLY_REVENUE | Marketing Analytics |
| L1/L7/L28 | Lookback activity windows: 1 day, 7 days, 28 days. Used for feature engagement metrics. | `*_COUNTS_L1, *_COUNTS_L7, *_COUNTS_L28 columns` | DIM_TEAMS_DAILY, DIM_USERS_DAILY | Data Science |
| M3 NRR | Net Revenue Retention measured at Month 3 post-acquisition for a cohort. OKR target: 82% to 90%. Cohort-based, NOT aggregate quarterly NRR. | `ARR_M3 / ARR_M0 for each monthly acquisition cohort` | FCT_TEAM_REVENUE_DAILY | Analytics |
| MAU | Monthly Active Users. Count of users with IS_ACTIVE_L28 = 1 on a given day — i.e., performed at least one qualifying action in the prior 28 days. Apollo uses L28 (rolling 28-day window) rather than calendar month. See Active User for qualifying action list. | `COUNT(*) WHERE IS_ACTIVE_L28 = 1` | INT_ACTIVE_USERS_DAILY, PRODUCT_METRICS_ACTIVE | Data Science |
| MRR | Monthly Recurring Revenue. Broken out by channel: SS (self-serve), SA (sales-assisted), rep, labs. | `MRR, MRR_SS, MRR_SA, MRR_REP, MRR_LABS` | FCT_DAILY_REVENUE | Finance / Analytics |
| MTTR | Mean Time To Resolution. Time from incident creation to resolution in PagerDuty. | `RESOLVED_AT - CREATED_AT` | APOLLO_INCIDENT_DATA | DevOps |
| Multi-product Activation | A team using 2+ Apollo product features (enrichment, email, dialer, meetings, AI). | `Derived from feature-level L7 counts` | DIM_TEAMS_DAILY | Product Analytics |
| NNARR | Net New ARR. New + expansion - churn - downgrade. The growth metric. | `Derived from ARR_CHANGE by CHANGE_CATEGORY` | FCT_MONTHLY_REVENUE | Finance |
| NRR | Net Revenue Retention. (Starting ARR + expansion - contraction - churn) / Starting ARR. Tracked at 3-month rolling for VSB/SMB. | `Derived from FCT_MONTHLY_REVENUE change categories` | FCT_MONTHLY_REVENUE | Analytics |
| Non-Core Account | VSB segment AND (employees = 0 OR employees IS NULL). Growth skewing toward non-core hurts NRR durability. Always filter to core for durable analysis. | `IS_CORE_ACCOUNT = FALSE or IS_CORE_ACCOUNT_IND = 0` | DIM_SALESFORCE_ACCOUNTS, LU_TEAM_ATTRIBUTES | Analytics |
| Paid WAT | Weekly Active Teams with active paid subscription. Denominator for multi-product and use case metrics. | `Derived: WAT WHERE ARR > 0` | FCT_TEAM_ACTIVITY_DAILY, FCT_TEAM_REVENUE_DAILY | Product Analytics |
| Parallel Dial | Apollo dialer feature allowing simultaneous calls to multiple prospects. | `PARALLEL_DIAL_SESSION_ID, PARALLEL_DIAL_ITEM_ID` | FCT_MONGO_PHONE_CALLS, FCT_TEAM_PHONE_CALLS_DAILY | Product |
| Parent Account | The top-level SF account that owns one or more Apollo teams. Revenue metrics should use parent account level for dedup. | `IS_PARENT_ACCOUNT = TRUE` | FCT_MONTHLY_REVENUE, FCT_DAILY_REVENUE | Analytics |
| Pricing Variant | The pricing/plan variant a team is on. Critical for credit migration analysis. | `PRICING_VARIANT column` | DIM_MONGO_TEAMS, LU_TEAM_ATTRIBUTES | Monetization |
| Product | Intentionally ambiguous — always clarify which lens before querying. Four interpretations: (1) Feature Area — what capability the team uses (Email, Dialer, Enrichment, AI, Prospecting); use FEATURE_TYPE in AGG_TEAM_CREDITS or USE_CASE_* in DIM_ACTIVE_TEAMS_DAILY. (2) Plan Edition — subscription tier (Free/Basic/Professional/Custom); use APOLLO_EDITION in FCT_MONGO_DAILY_TEAM_AUDIT_REPORTS. (3) Add-on Module — discrete purchasable product (Inbound Router, Dialer add-on at $149/mo); use ADDITIONAL_FEE_BY_SOURCE in FCT_MONGO_DAILY_TEAM_AUDIT_REPORTS. (4) Credit Product — credit type consumed (unified, export, direct dial, AI, waterfall); use FEATURE_TYPE in AGG_TEAM_CREDITS. DEFAULT: use Feature Area (Lens 1) when no context given. NEVER use raw PRODUCT_ID or PRICING_VARIANT for exec questions. When ambiguous ask: Are you asking about feature areas, plan tiers, or add-on products? | `See definition — varies by lens` | AGG_TEAM_CREDITS, DIM_ACTIVE_TEAMS_DAILY, FCT_MONGO_DAILY_TEAM_AUDIT_REPORTS | Analytics / Monetization |
| Record Action | A user action on an Apollo record (contact/account). Core unit of the F14D activation metric. Includes: email send, call made, sequence add, enrichment request. | `Derived from Amplitude events classified as record actions` | FCT_TEAM_ACTIVITY_DAILY, LU_AMPLITUDE_FEATURE_RULES | O&A (Adhiraj) |
| SQO | Sales Qualified Opportunity. An opportunity that has met qualification criteria. Tracked by count and date. | `IS_SQO = TRUE` | DIM_SALESFORCE_OPPORTUNITIES | Sales Ops |
| Subscription Period | Billing frequency classification. Values: month-to-month, annual, churn. Determines revenue recognition and retention cohort behavior. | `SUBSCRIPTION_PERIOD column` | FCT_DAILY_REVENUE | Finance |
| TOF Sales Bucket | Top-of-funnel lead source classification for opportunities. Other is typically excluded. | `TO_F_SALES_BUCKET <> 'Other'` | DIM_SALESFORCE_OPPORTUNITIES | Marketing |
| Team | An Apollo workspace/account unit. The atomic billing entity. Multiple users belong to one team. | `Identified by TEAM_ID / APOLLO_TEAM_ID` | DIM_MONGO_TEAMS, DIM_SALESFORCE_APOLLO_TEAMS, LU_TEAM_ATTRIBUTES | Product |
| Unified Credits | Apollo consolidated credit system replacing per-feature credits. Teams may or may not be migrated. | `PRODUCT_ID ILIKE '%unified%' or HAS_BEEN_MIGRATED_TO_UNIFIED_CREDIT_VARIANT = TRUE` | AGG_TEAM_CREDITS, DIM_MONGO_TEAMS | Monetization |
| Use Case | One of 4 Apollo product categories: Enrichment, GenPipe Traditional, GenPipe NextGen, Win/Close. Multi-product = using 2+ use cases. OKR target: 1.18 to 1.33 avg use cases per Paid WAT. | `USE_CASE_* and HAS_*_TEAM_USAGE_L1 columns` | DIM_ACTIVE_TEAMS_DAILY | Product Analytics |
| W4 Retention | Week-4 retention rate. Percent of teams in a weekly cohort that remain active at days 22-28 post-activation. Key AI product metric. | `Derived from weekly activity cohorts` | DIM_ACTIVE_TEAMS_DAILY, FCT_TEAM_ACTIVITY_DAILY | Product Analytics |
| WAT | Weekly Active Teams. A rolling 7-day metric — count of teams with at least one qualifying active user in the prior 7 days, sampled at a single point in time. CRITICAL GUARDRAIL: NEVER sum WAT across multiple dates — it is a rolling window metric and summing causes double-counting of teams active across multiple weeks. Always present as a point-in-time snapshot or weekly trend table (one row per week-end date). Paid WAT ~69K; Total WAT ~362K. | `metric = 'wat', activity_date = <snapshot_date> — pick ONE date, never SUM across dates` | ANALYTICS_DATASCIENCE.product_metrics_daily, DIM_TEAMS_DAILY (segmentation only) | Product Analytics |
| WAU | Weekly Active Users. Count of users with IS_ACTIVE_L7 = 1 on a given day — i.e., performed at least one qualifying action in the prior 7 days. See Active User for qualifying action list. | `COUNT(*) WHERE IS_ACTIVE_L7 = 1` | INT_ACTIVE_USERS_DAILY, PRODUCT_METRICS_ACTIVE | Data Science |
| Waterfall | Apollo data enrichment pipeline that tries multiple providers sequentially to enrich a contact. |  | DIM_MONGO_CONTACTS, DIM_MONGO_WATERFALL_STEP_RESULTS, FCT_TEAM_ENRICHMENT_DAILY | Data/Product |
| market_segment_tier | Product/in-app segmentation based on sales_department_size. Tier 1 (>=100), Tier 2 (20-99), Tier 3 (5-19), Tier 4 (<5 or freemail). DIFFERENT from account_segment. Controls waterfall activation, trial type, Sprig surveys. | `market_segment_tier method in leadgenie` | DIM_MONGO_TEAMS, DIM_ACTIVE_TEAMS_DAILY | Product |
