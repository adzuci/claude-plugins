## Mission

> "Make world-class go-to-market accessible to everyone."

Apollo's thesis: modern GTM (CRM + contact data + email automation + dialer + call recording + forecasting + analytics) has historically been inaccessible to most businesses. Apollo brought it together in one platform. The next era resets around AI-native orchestration — agents that don't just recommend, but **act**.

---

## The Horizon Framework

### Horizon 1 — Core Business (most work lives here)
**Theme:** Improve NRR. Fix time to value, expansion, and preventable churn. Achieve upmarket readiness with repeatable economics.

**FY27 H1 targets:**
- Improve VSB + SMB NRR by **≥10 points** via redesigned AI-first onboarding and journey
- Reduce preventable churn by **10%** via health scoring and lifecycle intervention
- Expansion revenue: **$115.7M** (upsell seats, credits, SKUs)
- MM + ENT new + upsell ARR: **$21.8M**
- Improve MM competitive win rates
- Reduce MM + ENT churn, improve GRR

**NRR improvement as a system (four levers):**
1. Reduce time to value — onboarding is a growth lever, not a support function
2. Drive org-wide expansion — admin/governance surfaces, rollout GTM motions
3. Increase credit/consumption monetization — legible, predictable, bounded
4. Raise product quality on core surfaces — data assets, dialer, call quality

**Top churn reasons (per VoC):**
- "Not using it enough" — dominant across all segments
- Role/responsibility change (36% of low usage churn)
- "Wasn't sure how to get value from Apollo" (19%)
- Product complexity is a first-order problem — UI/UX simplification is Horizon 1 work

**Why we lose deals:**
- Competitive preference, data quality, product gaps — by segment (ENT/MM/SMB/VSB)

---

### Horizon 2 — Proving Ground
**Theme:** Do these new products generate real adoption and repeatable revenue? Graduate to H1 or cut. No indefinite middle state.

**FY27 H2 targets:**
| Product | ARR Target | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Inbound | $6.4M | Defined activation + attach metrics required |
| Parallel Dialer | $4.1M | Call quality + connect rate must clear predefined bar |
| Conversational Intelligence | $2.4M | Either generates standalone revenue or becomes free/credit-monetized |
| AI Sheets | — | Win the GTM Engineer persona as power user data surface |

**Rule:** All H2 products must have explicit GTM pathways — PLG intersections, monetization, and sales-led motions. Multi-product does not sell itself.

---

### Horizon 3 — Future Platform
**Theme:** Turn Apollo from a collection of GTM tools into a single AI system that decides what should happen next and safely makes it happen across tools, roles, and workflows.

**FY27 H3 vision:**
- Apollo operates on a single shared understanding of accounts, contacts, activity, and signals
- No longer treats data, outbound, inbound, and follow-up as separate tools
- Platform sees the full account picture, decides next best action, executes automatically
- AI must **act**, not just recommend — reversible, rate-limited, observable

**Constraint:** H3 must not slow H1 or H2 execution. Investment is deliberate, centralized, and protected.

## Operating Principles ("Meeting the Moment")

1. **AI transformation** — not just AI features. Build agents that operate end-to-end and replace work entirely (not just speed it up). "Best talent will only accept working at AI-native companies within a year or two."
2. **Impact, Ownership, Mastery** — clear ownership, fast decisions, direct communication. Remote-first with intentional density.
3. **Brand clarity** — Apollo's external narrative: *"The system that turns GTM data into action, powered by AI, grounded in trusted execution, built for outcomes."* Stop being shy.
4. **Financial discipline** — Rule of 40 target, >5x LTV:CAC, invest only where there's clear ROI. Stop work that doesn't produce measurable outcomes.

## Analytics Implications

When doing any product debrief or metric analysis, frame findings against these horizons:
- **H1 lens:** Does this signal affect NRR, time to value, churn, or expansion? These are the primary metrics.
- **H2 lens:** Is Inbound / Dialer / CI on track to hit their ARR targets? Are activation metrics healthy?
- **H3 lens:** Is the platform moving toward unified account intelligence and agentic execution?

Key OKR anchors to always keep in mind:
- VSB + SMB NRR +10 points
- Preventable churn -10%
- Expansion ARR $115.7M
- MM + ENT new + upsell $21.8M
- Credits: 2,066M total (especially Waterfall +402%, AI Power-Up +151%)
