# Worked example: Growth Conversion, all KRs At Risk

An approved output showing the all-At-Risk pattern with full Problem/Diagnosis/Plan arcs. Notice the Story leads with the dominant pattern (mix-shift) plus an underlying signal that complicates the picture. The formatting standards apply to all sections.

```markdown
### The Story

Two weeks into Q2 and all three KRs are reading below target on the latest cohort. Mix-shift remains
the dominant story: VSB-Freemail's share of signups jumped from 73% to 85% in 8 weeks, and Basic
trial volume doubled under the v5 edition rollout. Underneath the blended numbers, non-freemail FTP
is essentially flat at 5.74% and Pro trial CVR has structurally improved to 4.5%.

One real within-segment watch item: VSB-Enriched, our highest-value self-serve segment, has softened
from 8.6% (3/02) to 6.6% (4/27). We're checking whether this ties to the Champion Onboarding
rollback or the April volume jump.

### What Changed and Why

**W2 FTP slipped to 1.22% for the 4/27 cohort, the third consecutive read below target, driven by
VSB-Freemail dilution.** Freemail share grew from 73% (3/02) to 85% (4/27) while within-segment
freemail conversion fell from 0.85% to 0.40%, suggesting the new freemail traffic is lower-intent
than historical freemail. Holding mix constant at March levels would put the blended rate at ~1.55%,
above target. Non-freemail blended is 5.74%, essentially flat.

**Trial W2 CVR fell to 3.73% for the 4/27 cohort, compressing toward the 3.5% floor.** The decline
is almost entirely in Basic, which fell from 7.5% (3/16) to 3.1% as Basic volume doubled to ~19K/week
under the v5 edition introduction. Pro CVR crossed above Basic for the first time at 4.5% (up from
2.96% in February), the Wizard removal payoff continuing to compound.

### Supporting KRs at a Glance

**KR1: W2 Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate (target 1.33%)** – **At Risk** – 1.22% on the 4/27 cohort,
third consecutive read below target.

* **Problem:** Blended rate has slipped -0.34pp in three weeks (1.56% → 1.22%).
* **Diagnosis:** Almost entirely mix-shift. VSB-Freemail share at 85% (up 12pp in 8 weeks),
  within-segment freemail rate down to 0.40%. Non-freemail blended is flat at 5.74%.
* **Plan:** Following up with growth marketing on the freemail acquisition mix and with activation
  on the Champion Onboarding rollback timing. Product levers in-flight: [Unified Pro Trial](url)
  (live), [Simplified Upgrade Modal](url) (5/26).

### What's Next

**Trial experience:** [Unified Pro Trial Experiment](url) and [Bulk Select Free Trial Badge](url)
ship 5/11. [Streamline End-of-Trial Experience](url) targets 5/21.

**Feature gates:** [Simplified Layout with Monthly Default](url) targets 5/12. [Extra-Large Buttons
on Standard Feature Gates](url) ships 5/27.
```
