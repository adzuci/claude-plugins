# **Onboarding & Activation**

## *How are we simplifying onboarding to drive faster activation and reduce time to first value?*

**DRI:** Nancy Shao

### Q1 Strategy

Collapse the gap between signup and first value by stripping friction from onboarding and surfacing the product's core loops faster.

**1. Decrease time to value in-product.** The onboarding wizard added 20+ screens before users touched anything useful. We're removing or replacing legacy flows with streamlined paths that get teams to record actions within minutes, not days.

**2. Amplify Hub & Home.** Hub and Home are the two surfaces where activated behavior compounds. We're consolidating them and increasing visibility so new users land in the right context from day one.

**3. Build challenger solutions.** Enrichment and AI-powered flows that meet users where they are, generating value before the user has to configure anything.

Each +1pp in Habit RA = $305K incremental ARR.

### The Story

Wizard removal is a confirmed win: stat sig lifts on both Habit RA (+8.6%) and FTP conversion (+8.2%), validating the thesis that fewer screens before value drives better outcomes. The tradeoff is real, though. Removing the wizard cut inbound TOFU WAU and collapsed VSB add-on upgrades from 12/week to 3/week, but the FTP lift more than offsets that on net paid conversion. Aggregate Habit RA still reads below baseline because treated cohorts are aging through the 14-day measurement window at a 50/50 split, so the full effect hasn't surfaced in the top-line number yet.

### What Changed and Why

Habit RA sits at 11.27%, below the 12% baseline and well short of the 17% target. The headline number is artificially suppressed: the wizard removal experiment ran at a 50/50 split, and treated cohorts are still maturing through the 14-day activation window. As those cohorts age in, the top-line metric should reflect the treatment group's actual performance.

**Wizard removal delivered stat sig results.** The experiment concluded with an +8.58% relative lift on Habit RA (p=0.020) and +8.21% relative lift on FTP conversion (p=0.0045) across 162K+ users per variant. These numbers come from Hex; Amplitude's calculation is miscalculated and shouldn't be referenced. The wizard was 20+ screens standing between signup and first value. Removing it got users into the product faster and that showed up in both activation and conversion.

**TOFU traffic eroded as a tradeoff.** Wizard removal reduced inbound TOFU WAU, with ~30% week-over-week erosion in add-on upgrade traffic. VSB upgrades dropped from 12/week to 3/week. The net math still works: the +8.2% FTP lift generates more paid conversion than the TOFU channel was producing. But the add-on path will need a separate solution.

### Supporting KRs at a Glance

**Habit RA: % SMB+ teams reaching 4+ record actions in 14 days (target 17%)** -- **Off Track** -- Currently ~11.27%, below the 12% baseline.

- **Problem:** Habit RA is 5.7pp below target and 0.7pp below baseline despite the wizard removal win.
- **Diagnosis:** The +8.6% relative lift from wizard removal is real but insufficient alone to close a 5pp gap. Treated cohorts are still aging through the 14-day window at 50/50 split, suppressing the aggregate number.
- **Plan:** Champion onboarding flow (target 3/19) is the next lever: an AI-powered flow that crawls the user's website and generates prospecting filters. Hub visibility improvements follow on 3/23.

**4-week retention: % SMB+ teams retained at week 4 (target 19.5%)** -- **Off Track** -- Currently ~16.03%, below the 17.5% baseline.

- **Problem:** Retention sits 3.5pp below target.
- **Diagnosis:** Retention is downstream of activation. Activated teams retain at 45.2% at week 4 vs. 2.3% for non-activated. Until Habit RA improves, retention won't move.
- **Plan:** Same levers as KR1. Improving activation is the fastest path to improving retention.

**3-month paid retention (target 69.3%)** -- **At Risk** -- Currently at 68.3% baseline. This is a lagging indicator and won't be readable until late in the quarter.

### What's Next

**Activation flows:** Champion onboarding flow targets 3/19. It's AI-powered: crawls the user's website, generates prospecting filters, and drops them into their first search. Hub visibility improvements target 3/23 to surface activation loops earlier.

**Home consolidation:** Home/Hub consolidation is in design, targeting 4/3 launch. This collapses two surfaces into one coherent landing experience for new users.

**Challenger bets:** Enrichment challenger is exploring auto-enriching newly signed-up users so they see value before they configure anything. AI challenger is in early discussion.
