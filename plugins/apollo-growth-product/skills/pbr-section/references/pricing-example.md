### The Story

Pricing is generating real incremental revenue on two fronts. The annual checkout banner experiment is projecting ~$500K+ annualized from a simple toggle change, and pending cancel reactivation has pulled ~400 teams back into paid plans. Both are early enough that durability is still the open question, but the signals are clean and the next iterations are already queued.

### What Changed and Why

Self-serve new ARR from pricing changes continues to trend positively, driven by two experiments generating measurable revenue.

**Annual checkout banner.** Annual-only purchases dipped, but total purchases increased, projecting ~$30K incremental revenue after 12 months (annualized ~$500K+). The experiment wraps next week. A revised version will show the annual/monthly toggle only to users who initially selected Monthly, targeting launch the week of Mar 16. [Amplitude experiment](https://app.amplitude.com/experiment/apollo-io/241072/config/830939/activity)

**Pending cancel reactivation.** Roughly 400 teams reactivated so far, generating ~$50K in purchases. That's a strong early signal. Retention durability is still under analysis, so the real question is how many of those teams stick past the first renewal cycle.
