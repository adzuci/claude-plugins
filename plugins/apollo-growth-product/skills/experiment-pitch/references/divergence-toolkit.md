# Divergence toolkit

Used in Step 3 of `experiment-pitch`, before any evaluation. The goal is 2-3 genuinely different
versions of the solution, not one idea plus two rewordings.

## Get out of evaluate mode first

The rational reflex kills ideas before they're any good. Flip each one:

| Rational reflex | Creative move |
|---|---|
| "This is the best idea, let's go." | Think again. |
| "Won't work because…" | Defer judgment. |
| "Too hard / don't know how." | Defer the *how*. |
| "We can have X or Y." | Why not both? |

## SCAMPER prompts

Run the idea through each prompt. Skip the ones that produce nothing rather than forcing a variant.

- **Substitute** — different trigger, surface, or audience
- **Combine** — merge with another feature or flow
- **Adapt** — borrow a pattern that works elsewhere in the product or market
- **Modify/Magnify/Minify** — bigger, smaller, louder, quieter
- **Put to another use** — same mechanism, different job
- **Eliminate** — remove a step instead of adding one
- **Reverse/Rearrange** — flip the order, invert the default
